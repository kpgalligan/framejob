# 7.7 Import

`MediaImporter.probe(url)` (async): loads `duration`, video/audio tracks,
`naturalSize` × `preferredTransform` (so rotated phone footage reports its
display size), `nominalFrameRate` (converted to the nearest rational
among the common rates, else `[round(fps × 1000), 1000]`), alpha
(`hasAlphaChannel` on the format description), and returns the `media[]`
descriptor for `add_media` — and for `relink_media`'s `probe`, so a relink
takes the new file's duration, size, rate and flags (§11, #63).
Thumbnails: 160 px tall, one per second up to 600, JPEG in
`cache/thumbs`. Peaks: mono float, 100 buckets/s, in
`cache/peaks`. Both run in the background after import and the timeline
redraws as they land — and both are SKIPPED when the cache's completion
marker matches the file (§5.6); a relinked, changed, partial or unmarked
cache is rebuilt. Image files (PNG/JPEG/HEIC) are probed with ImageIO
and become `kind: image` media.

Formats measured on 2026-10-01 on macOS 26.5.1 (25F80), Apple M2 Max, by
`tools/proof/formats_probe.py` — the product's own `MediaImporter.waitForProbe`
(no ffmpeg rewrap) and one decode of the first frame, sound buffer or image,
over 1 s, 128×72 ffmpeg-made files. **Opens** (probe and decode): MOV, MP4,
M4V, MPEG-TS and AVI containers; H.264, HEVC (`hvc1`), ProRes 422, MJPEG and
MPEG-4 Part 2 video; M4A (AAC and ALAC), ADTS AAC, MP3, WAV, AIFF, CAF, FLAC,
Ogg Opus and Ogg Vorbis audio. **Images** (ImageIO): PNG, JPEG, TIFF (RGB),
GIF, BMP and HEIC open. **Probe-only** — the probe answers, but the first
frame does not decode ("Cannot Decode") and `isPlayable` is false: VP9 in MP4
and AV1 in MP4. **Refused** (the probe throws `unreadable`): MKV (H.264, AV1)
and WebM (VP9 with Opus, VP8 with Vorbis) (§11). AV1 decode depends on
hardware: this host reports `VTIsHardwareDecodeSupported` false for AV1 (and
VP9), and its answer is AV1-in-MP4 probe-only and AV1-in-MKV refused; a host
with an AV1 hardware decoder may answer differently. WebP was not measured
(no encoder on the host).
