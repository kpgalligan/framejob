# 7.9d Agent — record_screen

The op tools, their parity check and lenient decoding are §7.9a; the app-state tools are §7.9e; the
agent lane, the helper, the assistant session and its tab are §7.9b; the graphics loop, the dev
tools and the transport are §7.9c.

`record_screen {seconds, source?, camera?, camera_format?, display? | window?, microphone?,
system_audio?, show_clicks?, include_framejob_windows?, keys?, cursor?, rate?, size?, place?, enhance?,
auto_zoom?, shortcut_overlays?, speed_up_typing?, camera_layout?, camera_cutout?, path?}` (road map 3 — File ▸ New
Recording…'s mirror: the file lands as ordinary media at the playhead of
the target document, or as an untitled document rated at `rate` when
nothing is open; it reads the Screen Recording and Microphone grants
synchronously and NEVER prompts, and its refusal order is the caller's own
mistakes, then the grants, then the display or window lookup, which cannot
be asked without them; pausable from the menu bar's item or File ▸ Pause
Recording while it runs, the result then carrying `pauses` and `segments`
— one entry per file, ALWAYS present — `duration` the TOTAL recorded, the
top-level keys the first segment's, and `unreadable` any segment left out,
an entry whose microphone file read carrying `mic_path` and `mic_duration`,
and `mic_media` and `mic_clip` when known, that voice landed in the slot its picture
would have had — spec §11 recording; `source: "voice_over"` is the panel's
Microphone Only row: the default microphone alone, `Voice-over <date> at
<time>.m4a` landing on one new back Microphone track, the screen-only
arguments and `microphone: false` refused by name and only the Microphone
grant read; `source: "camera"` (the panel's Source ▸ Camera row, spec §11 CAM-5) records a
camera alone, spec §11 CAM-3 and CAM-4: `camera` names one by its unique id or its name in any
case, else the default camera (the system's preferred, else the first built-in, else the first),
at CAM-3's best format or the one `camera_format` names (spec §11 CAM-6 (6): `1920x1080@30`, or
`WIDTHxHEIGHT` for that size's highest rate, or `best`; absent, blank or null is best), into
`Camera Recording <date> at <time>.mov` landed by the picture rule; CAM-3's refusal order —
`camera` and `camera_format` refused with source voice_over right after `source`, and with
`camera` the screen's arguments and `rate` refused together, then `microphone`, `project`, the
camera lookup (an unknown one named with every camera present), the format (one the camera lacks
named with its formats) and only then the Camera grant, read and never asked; the span counted
from the first frame; the answer's `camera: {name, id, format, problem?, note?}`; and with
source `screen`, spec §11 CAM-10 (the panel's Record the camera too box with a display or a
window, spec §11 CAM-11), a `camera` records that camera BESIDE the screen — one
`<movie stem> (Camera).mov` per segment, landed in the same undo step on ONE new front track
named Camera, each clip at its screen clip's start plus that segment's `camera_offset` (floored
at the screen clip's start and the previous camera clip's end, the head trimmed below it) and
laid out in `camera_layout` (road map creator-jobs CO8, spec §11 CO-80: `corner_square`, the
default, `corner_rounded`, `corner_circle`, `side_by_side`, `camera_full`; the panel's Layout
pop-up) by ONE `switch_layout {camera, screen: the segment's screen clip, layout, shape, corner:
bottom_right, at: the clip's start, duration: one canvas frame, through: max(the screen clip's
end, the camera clip's end)}`, so `corner_square` is the bottom-right corner, a quarter of the
canvas wide and a third tall inside the 90 % safe-area ring, and a screen whose clip time at the
camera's start is past 0 keeps its base with one one-frame layout action there; a segment whose
screen minted no clip, or whose screen clip does not play at the camera's start, takes that
corner by one `set_video {x, y, scale}` instead; `camera_cutout: true` (CO-81, the panel's
Remove the camera's background when it lands box) then runs Remove Background — one base
`set_video {cutout}` per camera clip that can be cut out — as its own undo step Remove
Background right after the landing and before any `enhance` and the polish, the answer gaining
`camera_cutout: {clips}` or `camera_cutout_error` ("No camera clip landed on the timeline, so
Remove Background did not run."); `camera_format`, `camera_layout` and `camera_cutout` without
`camera` refused ("With source screen, <arg> needs camera — name the camera to record beside the
screen."), strangers with voice_over and camera (CO-83 (a)), and `camera_layout` outside its
vocabulary then, with `place: false`, a `camera_layout` given ("camera_layout lays out the landed
camera clips, so it needs place: true.") and a `camera_cutout: true` ("camera_cutout: true
removes the landed camera clips' background, so it needs place: true.") refused after the
polish and before any grant (CO-83 (b)); the camera lookup, its format and the Camera grant read BEFORE the Screen
Recording grant; each `segments[k]` gaining `camera_path`, `camera_duration`, `camera_offset`,
`camera_problem`, `camera_note`, `camera_media` and `camera_clip` as they apply; a new
project's remainder step named Add Camera or Add Camera and Microphone for one segment; the
stop wait three probes per segment and its timeout naming the camera files; and `enhance`,
spec §11 AU-68 and AU-69 (the panel's Enhance the microphone when it lands box), boolean,
default false, read right after `place` and before any grant — `enhance: true` without a
microphone refused first ("enhance: true enhances the microphone's clip, so it needs
microphone: true (or source voice_over)."), then with `place: false` ("enhance: true needs
place: true — with place: false the microphone lands as media with no clip to enhance.") — runs,
after the unchanged landing, `enhance_voice`'s own body at −16 LUFS over the recording's voice
clips once their loudness and speech caches are current (polled every 0.5 s, given up at 300 s),
as its own undo step Enhance Voice; the answer gains `enhance` (`enhance_voice`'s answer) or
`enhance_error` (its refusal, "No microphone clip landed on the timeline, so there was nothing
to enhance.", or "Enhance Voice did not run on the recording: its microphone was not ready to
measure within 5 minutes. Call enhance_voice with its clips."), and `project` is read after it).
Every display, window or picked recording writes its cursor beside each segment movie as
`<stem>.fjcursor` (road map creator-jobs CU1b1, spec §11 CU-11…CU-13, CU-18): each `segments[k]`
gains `cursor_path` and `cursor_image` (`"none"`) when that segment's file was written, and
`cursor_problem` when it was not (no E1, or the writer's sentence) or holds only the geometry the
screen had when the segment began (the frame watch lost); every segment's problem is also in the
note, and none fails the recording. No argument, op or `rc_*` is added.

**The polish on landing** (road map creator-jobs CU10, spec §11 CU-100…CU-102; the panel's "When
it lands:" boxes Zoom in on clicks, Show shortcuts and Speed up typing): `auto_zoom`,
`shortcut_overlays` and `speed_up_typing`, booleans, default false, read right after `enhance` and
before any grant, each in that order refused with `place: false` ("auto_zoom: true plans on the
landed screen clips, so it needs place: true.", "shortcut_overlays: true places overlays over the
landed screen clips, so it needs place: true.", "speed_up_typing: true speeds up the landed screen
clips' typing, so it needs place: true.") and the last two then without `keys: "shortcuts"`
("shortcut_overlays: true needs keys: \"shortcuts\" — without it the cursor file records no
shortcut to show.", "speed_up_typing: true needs keys: \"shortcuts\" — without it the cursor
file records no typing to speed up."); with `voice_over` or `camera` each is a stranger refused by
name with the other screen-only arguments. After the unchanged landing and after any `enhance`,
each runs its tool's own body (`auto_zoom`, `add_shortcut_overlays`, `speed_up_typing`) at the
tools' defaults with no `from` or `to`, over the screen clips — the open container's clips whose
media is a segment's movie and has a cursor file, recomputed at every 0.5 s check — as its own
undo step Auto Zoom, Add Shortcut Overlays and Speed Up Typing, in that order. The speed-up waits
until `SpeedUpTyping.resolve` no longer waits for speech (its first wait starts the cache pass);
each is given up 300 s after its clock starts, which is when the one before it ran. The answer
gains each command's own answer under its name or a sentence under `<name>_error`: "No screen clip
with a cursor file landed on the timeline, so <title> did not run.", "<title> did not run on the
recording: the project was busy for 5 minutes. Call <name> with its clips." (the speed-up's: "its
speech was not detected within 5 minutes."), "record_screen was stopped before <title> ran, so it
did not run.", or the tool's own refusal; `project` is read after them. No op, `rc_*` or format is
added.

**`keys`** (road map creator-jobs CU6, spec §11 CU-60, CU-62; the panel's Record shortcuts box):
`off` (the default) or `shortcuts`, an enum so a later mode needs no rename. With `shortcuts` a
display, window or picked recording's cursor files hold each shortcut (⌘ or ⌃ held, or ⌥ with an
arrow, Delete, Forward Delete, Return, Enter, Tab or Esc), spelled `cmd`, `ctrl`, `opt`, `shift`
then the key, with its key code, and a time-only typed mark for every other key-down and every
auto-repeat; nothing while any process holds secure input; a typed key's character is never
read. The status item shows its keyboard mark and its "Recording keyboard shortcuts" row (CU-61).
Its refusals, in the order: `keys` with source `voice_over` or `camera` refused by name with the
other screen-only arguments; a value outside `off`, `shortcuts` refused with the vocabulary ("keys
must be one of off, shortcuts (got "all").") among the caller's own mistakes, after
`include_framejob_windows`; then, after the Microphone grant and before `project`, the Input
Monitoring grant (`CGPreflightListenEventAccess()`), read and never requested, refused with
"Input Monitoring is not granted to Frame Job. Turn it on in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security
▸ Input Monitoring." plus `launchClause`. The recorder itself refuses with the same sentence when
the grant is absent or its key tap cannot be made. System Settings is never opened.

**`cursor`** (road map creator-jobs CU3c, spec §11 CU-31, CU-36; the panel's Cursor popup):
`separate` or `in_picture`. With `separate` ScreenCaptureKit draws neither the pointer nor the
click ring (`showsCursor` and `showMouseClicks` false, the pixel format left `420v`), the cursor
file's header says `in_picture: false`, and Frame Job draws the cursor on the landed clip; with
`in_picture` the stream is configured as before CU3c, byte for byte. Absent means `separate` for
every screen source (CU-36, amended at CU0d). `show_clicks` keeps its meaning per mode: the
ring drawn into the movie In the picture; with `separate`, the landing adds ONE `set_video
{cursor: {clicks: "ripple"}}` over the placed screen clips whose media's cursor is not in the
picture, inside the landing's undo step (a new project's remainder step, named Cursor Clicks
when nothing else is in it). Its refusals: refused by name with source `voice_over` or `camera`,
after `keys`, with the other screen-only arguments; a value outside the vocabulary refused
("cursor must be one of separate, in_picture (got "x").") among the caller's own mistakes, after
`keys`'. A Separate segment whose cursor file was not written has no pointer anywhere, and its
`cursor_problem` ends "The pointer is not in the movie either: it was recorded Separate."

**A recording's cursor file after it lands** (road map creator-jobs CU5, §11 CU-50, CU-53;
`AgentServer+Cursor.swift`, `AgentCatalog+Cursor.swift`). The op tool `set_media_cursor {media,
cursor: {path, bookmark?, in_picture} | null}` is the Media tab's Cursor section ▸ Locate… ("Locate
Cursor File") and Remove ("Remove Cursor File"). A non-null `cursor` is checked as Locate… checks
it before the op: the file opens through the core and its `movie_size` is the movie's (CU-34's
sentences, "The cursor file wrong.fjcursor was not attached: its movie_size is 320 × 180 and the
movie is 640 × 360."), then "The cursor file A.fjcursor was not attached: its in_picture is false
and the call says true."; then the op's own rules (§6). `get_cursor_events {media | clip, from?,
to?, kinds?, smoothing?}` reads the file, off main: `kinds` ⊆ clicks, scrolls, shortcuts, typing,
path (the four without path by default); with `media` the times are the file's forward source
ticks, default `[0, duration)`; with `clip` they are clip ticks, default `[0, duration)`, mapped
through the clip's source map on a ramp, else `in + t × speed`, each event kept inside `[from,
to)`, and each click and scroll gains `q = (x − W/2 − (l − r)/2, y − H/2 − (t − b)/2)`, its place
in the clip's region frame (W × H the media's size, the crop the clip's base crop, or
`clip_state`'s at the event when it has actions). The answer is `{ok, media, clip?, time, from, to,
size, cursor_file, in_picture, clicks?: [{t, button, down, count, x, y, q?}], scrolls?: [{t, x, y,
dx, dy, q?}], shortcuts?: [{t, keys, code}], typing?: [{from, to, marks}], path?: {smoothing, t, x,
y}, count, next?, project}`, x and y in movie pixels, the path sampled every 60,000 source ticks at
`smoothing` (the clip's base `video.cursor.smoothing` by default, else 0). Rows of every kind and
path samples are merged by time and paged at 5,000: `next` is the first time left out, and a page
holds only earlier times. It takes no `scope`: a clip is looked up in the container `set_scope` has
open. Refused in order: not exactly one of media and clip; `kinds`; `smoothing` outside 0–1; an
unknown id; not a media clip; a reversed clip ("Clip C plays reversed; get_cursor_events reads only
clips that play forward. Ask with media M for the file's own times."); no cursor file; the file
missing (naming Locate… and `set_media_cursor`); `to` ≤ `from`; the core's sentence for a file it
will not open.

**Auto Zoom** (road map creator-jobs CU7, §11 CU-70…CU-76; `AgentServer+AutoZoom.swift`,
`AgentCatalog+AutoZoom.swift`). `auto_zoom {clip | clips, zoom?, lead?, stay?, follow?, typing?,
replace?, motion_blur?, from?, to?}` is Clip ▸ Auto Zoom…: the clips' cursor files read off main, then the core's
`plan_zooms` (`rc_cursor_query`, one track per clip, the edited project) applied as one undo step
"Auto Zoom" of `remove_action` (with `replace`), `set_video` (a later half's base) and
`add_zoom_action` ops, then one `set_video {motion_blur}` over the planned clips whose base
`motion_blur` is 0 (road map creator-jobs CU11b, §11 CU-118 (c), (e): the sheet's Motion blur;
none when it is 0). Defaults: zoom 2 (1.25–4), lead 0.6 s (0.2–2), stay 2.5 s (0.5–10), follow
and typing true, replace false, motion_blur 0.5 (0–1); `from` and `to` are container ticks. It takes no `scope`: the clips
are looked up in the container `set_scope` has open. The answer is `{ok, visits, added, replaced,
dropped, closed_at_cut, bases_set, actions: [{clip, at, duration, kind}], blurred, project}` (`at` in clip
ticks, `kind` in, pan, follow or back); with nothing to plan, `ok` with a `note` and no undo step.
Refused in order: not exactly one of clip and clips; a range ("Zoom is 1.2; it runs 1.25–4.");
`to` ≤ `from`; an unknown id; not a media clip; no cursor file; the file missing; the project or
scope changed while the files were read; then the core's sentences, capitalised — a clip named
twice, reversed ("Clip C plays reversed; Auto Zoom plans only clips that play forward."), no
picture, placed ("Clip C is placed smaller than the canvas; a zoom would fill the canvas. Nest it
(Clip ▸ Nest) and run Auto Zoom inside the nest.").

**Shortcut overlays** (road map creator-jobs CU8, §11 CU-80…CU-84;
`AgentServer+ShortcutOverlays.swift`, `AgentCatalog+ShortcutOverlays.swift`).
`add_shortcut_overlays {clip | clips, from?, to?, duration?, position?, theme?}` is Clip ▸ Add
Shortcut Overlays: the clips' cursor files read off main, then the core's `plan_overlays`
(`rc_cursor_query`, one track per clip, the edited project) applied as one undo step "Add
Shortcut Overlays" of an optional `add_track` (a new front "Shortcuts" track) and one
`add_graphic_clip` per overlay, drawn by the project's module whose text equals the bundled
`keyboard-keys.js` (reused, else the template written once, `written`). Defaults: duration 1.5 s
(0.5–10), position `bottom` (`center`, `top`), theme `dark` (`light`); `from` and `to` are
container ticks. An overlay lasts at least D_min(n) = 10n + 25 frames for n keycaps; the same
shortcut again extends it, labelled "×k"; a different one too close is dropped. It takes no
`scope`: the clips are looked up in the container `set_scope` has open. The answer is `{ok,
added, dropped, merged, track, new_track, module, written, overlays: [{at, duration, keys,
label}], project}` (`at` in container ticks), with `note` "N shortcuts came too close to show."
when any was dropped; with nothing to place, `ok` with the note "No shortcut was recorded in the
selected clips' played windows, so nothing was added.", no undo step and no module written.
Refused in order: not exactly one of clip and clips; `duration` out of range ("Duration is 0.2 s;
it runs 0.5–10."); a `position` or `theme` not in its list; `to` ≤ `from`; an unknown id; not a
media clip; no cursor file; the file missing; the project or scope changed while the files were
read; then the core's sentences, capitalised — a clip named twice, reversed ("Clip C plays
reversed; Add Shortcut Overlays places only clips that play forward.").

**Speed Up Typing** (road map creator-jobs CU9b, §11 CU-91…CU-93;
`AgentServer+SpeedUpTyping.swift`, `AgentCatalog+SpeedUpTyping.swift`).
`speed_up_typing {clip | clips, rate?, shortest?, keep_pitch?, from?, to?}` is Clip ▸ Speed Up
Typing…: the clips' cursor files read off main, then the core's `plan_typing` (`rc_cursor_query`,
one track per clip, the edited project) — each typing stretch through S, less every speech span
of a media clip with sound under it and each shortcut's overlay span, every clip's pieces united,
then `shortest` and the 0.3 s margins — answered as `retime_range` ops right to left and applied
as one undo step "Speed Up Typing", after the `set_media_speech` ops the speech step makes for a
counted media with no stored spans (its current voice-activity cache; refused when its file is
offline or detection is unavailable; while detection runs the call starts the pass and is
refused "{name}'s speech has not been detected yet. Try again in a moment."). Defaults: rate 4
(2–8), shortest 3 s (1–30), keep_pitch true; `from` and `to` are container ticks. It takes no
`scope`. The answer is `{ok, stretches, short, ranges: [{from, to, sped_to}], saved,
speech_saved, project}` plus `new_clips`, and `audio_left_out` / `audio_note` read on the
retime's baseline when a clip with sound crosses the audio ceiling; with nothing to speed, `ok`
with a `note`, no undo step. Refused in order: not exactly one of clip and clips; an unknown id;
not a media clip; no cursor file; the file missing; the project or scope changed while the files
were read; then the core's sentences, capitalised — rate ("Rate is ×9; it runs ×2–×8."),
shortest, `to` ≤ `from`, a clip named twice, reversed ("Clip C plays reversed; Speed Up Typing
speeds only clips that play forward."), and any range `retime_range` refuses on a trial ("The
typing from A to B ticks cannot be sped up: …"), nothing changed.
