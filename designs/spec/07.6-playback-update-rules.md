# 7.6 Playback update rules

After every edit, `ProjectDocument` posts a change notification with the
new `ReelProject` and `isLive`. `Playback` then:

1. Sets `RecipeSource.current = newProject` (an atomic reference the
   compositor reads on every request).
2. Computes the plan JSON; if it equals the current plan, the edit was
   **property-only**: no rebuild — if paused, force one composed frame.
   The standard trick for that, a zero-tolerance seek to the time the
   player is already at, is a NO-OP on macOS 26.5.1 (measured by
   `tools/proof/preview_refresh_probe.py`: the completion fires and no
   composition request is issued; re-assigning the item's
   `videoComposition` is the same no-op), so `Playback.refreshPausedFrame`
   seeks one tick off the playhead — inside the same frame — and queues the
   return, landing back on the playhead's own tick with the new recipe
   drawn. Parked at the end, where the end tick composes the empty canvas,
   it rests on the last frame's final tick instead. A refresh asked for
   while a seek is in flight is run when the seek lands, never dropped.
3. Otherwise the edit was **structural**: rebuild the composition on a
   background queue, swap `AVPlayerItem` at the same time, keep the play
   state. Rebuilds are debounced at 80 ms during live gestures
   (`isLive`), and the final `endLiveEdit` always rebuilds if needed.

   Neither the refresh's round trip nor the rebuild's swap and re-land is
   reported as a playhead move: `Playback.onTimeChanged` carries seeks
   someone asked for and time while playing, and a restore that comes to
   rest somewhere other than the tick last reported reports that once (the
   end-parked rest, a timeline shortened under the playhead). A scope change
   is a restore on the NEW item (`Playback.seekAfterRebuild(to:)`): its time
   is the new container's, so the outgoing item is neither sought nor heard,
   and the rebuild lands there, heard once. If that rebuild fails, or the
   new container turns out empty, the outgoing item is dropped rather than
   left on the player, and the time is still heard once. An asked seek that
   arrives while a restore is seeking is heard once, when it lands. A plan
   back to the installed one discards a rebuild still in flight. While
   paused, an observer callback is heard only for the tick the player is at
   or seeking to, and only when it differs from the last report — a late
   callback for a superseded seek or a swapped-out item is dropped — and
   every seek's landing reports its rest once when it differs (#204, #209).
4. Instructions carry the plan's instruction ranges only; the
   compositor's `RecipeSource` is what makes property edits render
   without a rebuild.
5. A gated, levelled or de-essed clip's thresholds are relative to its measured
   loudness (§11 AU-41), and a levelled clip's curve is made from that
   loudness and its speech (§11 AU-66), so the audio DSP compared above
   changes when either lands with no edit at all: `AudioDynamicsLevels` hears
   the `loudness` and `speech` cache posts (a landing or a withdrawal), drops
   that media's memo and posts `.reversedMediaDidChange` for each open
   document with a gate, leveller or de-esser on the media, and the editor
   rebuilds as it does for a landed reversed copy (§11 AU-42).
6. A cut-out clip's person masks are made when the clip asks (§11 CO-22):
   `ensureRampAudio()` ends with `ensureMasks()`, so after every edit, the
   open, a landed reverse and a settled ramp (never inside a live gesture)
   the document wants, at once, the analysed ranges its cut-out clips'
   windows need — every plan video segment of a clip whose base
   `video.cutout` is set, in `project` and, with a nest open,
   `editedProject` — under one lane owner per document, sharing a running
   job that still meets the need rather than restarting it. Until a frame's
   mask is cached the clip draws uncut (§11 CO-6). Each publish posts
   `.analysisDidPublish`, and the editor whose cache folder it names
   refreshes the paused frame by the round trip above; the plan did not
   change, so nothing rebuilds, and the compositor re-reads the pack on its
   marker's stat (§11 CO-25).

Scrubbing: the timeline coalesces to one in-flight `seek(to:toleranceBefore:.zero,toleranceAfter:.zero)`
at a time, dropping intermediates. The scrub's sound is not the player's:
`ScrubAudio` reads 80 ms grains from a composition built only for
scrubbing, remade when the player's composition object changes, and plays
them through an `AVAudioEngine` of its own (§11 AU-130…AU-134).
