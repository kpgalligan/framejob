# 7.9j Agent — the media analysis tools

The app-state tools are §7.9e; this file holds the three that read or want a media's on-device
analysis: `get_media_analysis`, `split_at_shots` (the mirror of Clip ▸ Split at Shot Changes) and
`analyze_cutout`. They were §7.9e's until #448 (2026-11-09), moved here unchanged because that
file had passed the spec's ~500-line split rule, and they keep the commas of its list: each
paragraph is one item of §7.9e's run of tools, and still ends in the list's comma.

`get_media_analysis {media | clip, kind, from?, to?}` (road map "creator-jobs"
CJ2a, §11 CJ-20…CJ-25) → an analysis of one media, or of the part of it one
clip plays, made on this Mac on the analysis lane (`AnalysisLane`) and kept in
`cache/analysis/<kind>/` (§5.6), `get_transcript`'s counterpart for pictures and
sounds; `kind` `shots` (CJ-24) answers the hard cuts, `masks` (road map CO1,
§11 CO-10…CO-14) a person's masks' runs, `faces` (road map CJ3, §11
CJ-30…CJ-34) every face per sample, `sound_events` (road map FC3, §11
FC-30…FC-34) six groups' confidence per window of sound. Exactly one of `media` and
`clip`; `from` and `to` are integer forward-media source ticks, not snapped,
defaulting to the media's whole length or, with `clip`, to the clip's played
source window and held inside it (`AnalysisClipTimes`: its in, speed or source
map, and the reversed mirror). One main-thread hop resolves the document, the
arguments, the cache folder, the file and the request; a window the store
covers with its read margin answers at once (`cached: true`), otherwise the
call wants the missing ranges under an owner of its own and waits for the job,
holding the agent lane's lock as `get_transcript` does, at most
`AnalysisLane.callWaitLimit` (60 s, CJ-S3). It answers `ok`, `kind`, `media`,
`clip` (with `clip`), `from`, `to`, `size` (the upright display size),
`cached`, `stamp` (the marker's, or null with `canned: true`), `run`
(`queued_at`, `started_at`, `ended_at` in process uptime, only when it ran a
job), `count`, `cuts` (`{frame, source}`, and with `clip` `clip_time`, ticks
from the clip's start in its playing direction, and `timeline`, its start plus
`clip_time`) and `project`; for `masks`, in place of `count` and `cuts`:
`mask_size` ([w, h], the pack footer's, the size Vision answered; null with no
pack), `frames` (the frames held in the window), `subject_found` (any run's),
`count` and `runs` (`{from, to, subject_found, frames}` in source ticks, a run
the maximal span of consecutive analysed frames in the window and one coverage
range whose masks agree on `found`, `to` its last frame's pts plus one frame) —
never pixels. A `masks` job reads a converting media through CO-7's third arm
(`AnalysisFrames+InputPass.swift`), every frame's own time kept. For `faces`,
in place of `count` and `cuts`: `rate` (10 samples a second), `count` and
`samples`, one row per covered sample in the window — `source` (the sample's
instant, `ticks(k)`), `frame` (the pts of the first kept frame at or after it,
within one sample, or null), `faces` in CJ-31's order (`box` [x, y, w, h], 0…1
of the upright picture from its top-left; `confidence`; `roll` and `yaw` in
radians; `mouth_gap` and `mouth_width`, the inner lips' height and outer lips'
width over the box's height), and with `clip` `clip_time` and `timeline` from
`source`. Every float is rounded to 4 decimals in the answer only, written as a
decimal number, null where Vision gave none; the cache stays raw. A one-face
row is 176 bytes of JSON (221 with `clip`, CJ-34's measure), so a call holds
about 296 s of samples (236 s with `clip`), less with more faces: an agent reads
a longer media in `from`/`to` windows of at most that. A `faces` job reads
through the standard arms at long side 720, on the Neural Engine (CJ-32).
For `sound_events`, in place of `count` and `cuts`: `window` and `hop` (900,000
and 450,000 ticks), `groups` (`{name, labels, first_class, threshold}`, every
group weak and `threshold` null until S-EV, FC-30), `count` and `windows`, one
row per covered window whose `source` (k × hop) is in [from, to) — `source` and
`laughter`, `applause`, `cheering`, `crowd`, `music`, `speech`, each rounded to
4 decimals, null where Sound Analysis gave none (a window overhanging the
sound's end) — and with `clip` `clip_time` and `timeline`. Its job reads the
first audio track through `speechReader`'s 16 kHz decode of each missing range,
past an MP3's encoder delay, on the sound queue (FC-31); a reversed clip is
refused, and so is a media with no sound, an audio-only media accepted.
For `subjects` (road map "creator-jobs" VR1, §11 VR-10…VR-14), `faces`' `rate`
(10, every instant a `faces` one), `count` and `samples`, each row `source`,
`frame`, `people` and `salient` (each `[{box, confidence}]`, `box` as `faces`',
in CJ-31's order; a person box covers the head and shoulders, `upperBodyOnly`),
with `clip_time` and `timeline` under `clip`; every float through
`answerNumber`, 4 decimals. A row with one person and one salient box is 177
bytes (222 with `clip`): about 294 s a call (235 s with `clip`). A `subjects`
job reads as `faces`' does, both requests on the Neural Engine (VR-11). View ▸
Show Subjects draws what `faces` and `subjects` hold and never makes them.
Refused, in
this order: not exactly one of `media`
and `clip`; an unknown id; not a media clip; for `sound_events` a clip that
plays in reverse ("… so its sound events are not read: its sound plays
backwards."); a media with no moving picture (a picture kind) or no sound
(`sound_events`, "Media M has no sound, so it has no sound_events to find.");
the file missing; `to` ≤ `from`; the wait past its bound ("… covered S s of the
window's W s …; call again to continue", the published ranges kept); `cuts` or
`runs` or `samples` or `windows` over 512 KiB of JSON, naming the largest `to`
that fits (for `samples` and `windows`, the `source` of the first row that does
not); a mask pack, a `samples.bin` or a `windows.bin` that does not parse,
naming why. On device: no network, no
key, no AI-7 row. It edits nothing and makes no undo step,
`split_at_shots {clips?}` (road map "creator-jobs" CJ2b, §11 CJ-28, CJ-29) — the
mirror of Clip ▸ Split at Shot Changes, through the same planner (`ShotSplit`)
→ the targets (media clips of a video file with their picture on; `clips`
absent, every one on an unlocked track in the open scope; any other named clip
under `skipped`) split at every `shots` cut inside their played windows, one
request per media over the union of the windows, each cut mapped through
`AnalysisClipTimes` to timeline time on the canvas grid, a cut within one
canvas frame of an edge dropped, one `split` per cut, latest first, as ONE undo
step "Split at Shot Changes". It runs off the main thread as
`get_media_analysis` does and waits for a missing analysis with no sheet, at
most 60 s; it is refused before any split past the clip ceiling, each split
counted with the linked partner it also cuts, on the unscoped project ("… would
add N clips … M more clips would fit, out of 4096."), and when the project or
scope changed while it waited. It answers `ok`, `cuts` (`[{clip, at}]`,
timeline ticks), `added`, `skipped`, `cached` and the edit's ids and `project`;
with no cut, `added: 0`, a `note` and no undo step,
`analyze_cutout {clips?, wait?}` (road map "creator-jobs" CO3, §11 CO-31) — the
mirror of the Clip tab's Picture ▸ Background section's Status and Analyze Now
→ for each named clip, or every cut-out clip in the open scope in timeline
order — a picture's too, since CO10b (§11 CO-108, CO-103 lifted) — the masks wanted again and a failed or stopped job run again
(`analyzeMasksNow()`), then the statuses read every 0.25 s until every clip
settles, `wait` seconds pass (0 when absent, at most 60) or the assistant's
Stop — never a timeout refusal. Off the main thread, no `scope`. It answers
`ok`, `clips` (`[{clip, media, state, progress, quality, frames, covered,
ready, subject_found, centroid, error?}]`: `state` `analyzing`, `ready`,
`no_person`, `no_subject` (a picture with subject `foreground` whose mask found
none), `failed` or `no_picture`; `frames` the needed grid indices of the
clip's windows (1 for a picture) and `covered` those stored; `centroid` the mean value-weighted
centre of the found masks in the media's upright pixels, null unless ready;
`error` "Clip N's person masks could not be made: <why>.", a foreground
picture's "Clip N's foreground masks could not be made: <why>.") and `project`.
Refused in order: `wait` outside 0…60, an unknown clip, a named clip with no
cutout, none cut out in the open scope. On device: no network, no key. It edits
nothing and makes no undo step,
