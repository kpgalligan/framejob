# 4.2 Preview canvas

- The composition at document size, centered in the void
  (`DS.canvasVoid`) with a 24 pt margin; the canvas background is the
  document's background color. Zoom with ⌘= / ⌘- / ⌘0 (fit) or pinch;
  Space-drag pans when zoomed in.
- Shows the frame at the playhead. During playback the frames come from
  the player; while scrubbing or editing, from exact seeks.
- The size caption under the video is mono 10 pt in `DS.textFaint` with
  0.06em tracking, matching every other machine-produced number in the app.
- **Selection**: click picks the topmost visible clip under the cursor at
  the current time (locked or hidden tracks are not hit). ⇧-click
  extends. Clicking empty canvas clears. Hit-testing uses each layer's
  canvas rect (§5.3) rotated by its rotation. A matte clip (§11 MT-7) is
  hit, selected and dragged like any clip — topmost inside its box, though
  it draws nothing there — so the clip it cuts is hit only outside it.
- **Handles** on the selected clip, drawn in canvas space through the
  clip's scale and rotation: eight scale handles (corners keep aspect,
  ⇧ frees it; edges scale one axis), a rotation handle above the top
  edge, drag inside to move, ⌥ scales about the center. Arrow keys nudge
  1 px, ⇧-arrows 10 px.
  **The handles follow the mode** (§11 CI-13): with an action selected, a
  move, scale, rotate or crop drag and an arrow-key nudge start from that
  action's END state and write it through `set_action_end`, as the
  inspector's fields do — the box on screen is still the frame at the
  playhead, and the drag moves the end state by the pointer's travel. A
  multi-selection writes the base, and so does a shape's box scale, whole:
  `set_shape` has no action form and the placement that pins its pivot
  travels with it.
- **Obscure regions** (§11 CI-16): a selected clip's regions are outlined
  dashed, with a centre grip and eight resize handles (⌥ about the centre);
  the layer's handles win, a drag inside moves the clip, and a target list
  of another length has no grips. A drag is one `set_video` or
  `set_action_end` and one undo step; Esc cancels any canvas drag.
- **Crop mode** (C, or double-click a video clip): the handles become
  crop edges in source space; the uncropped source ghosts at 30 %.
  Esc or Return leaves.
- **Region mode** (Z, or the Video sections' "Zoom to Region…" and the
  Obscure section's "Place on Canvas"): a modal overlay over the canvas on
  which a rectangle is dragged free — no aspect lock, which is what makes
  `fill` reachable at all. While a ZOOM drag runs, the rectangle that will
  actually end up filling the canvas is stroked dashed in the same ink at a
  lower alpha — larger than the band under `fit`, smaller under `fill` — so
  what you will get is on screen. Release commits: a zoom action at the playhead
  (⇧ chooses fill over fit) or an appended `video.obscure` region,
  whichever armed the mode. Z toggles, Esc cancels, and moving the
  selection ends it; the mode refuses to arm at all when there is no
  single selected clip on the canvas at the playhead, since there would be
  nothing to draw a rectangle over. A band under 6 px on either axis beeps
  and leaves the mode armed — a stray click is a miss, not a decision. The
  rectangle is read in §5.3's source-pixel frame through all four of its
  corners, so a rotated layer gets the right box. Arming ANY modal canvas
  mode ends whatever other one was armed — the eyedropper, the qualifier's
  Pick from Canvas and the region band are one overlay each and only one may
  be up, or a drag begun for one commits the other's edit.
- **Pick from Canvas for the qualifier** (grading-2 G2-4, §11 GR-13): the
  Qualifier section's button arms the third mode, over adjustment clips only
  (otherwise a beep). It wears the crosshair and the pill "Click to pick the
  colour to qualify · Esc to cancel"; one click on the picture samples the
  frame rendered with the clip and every canvas layer in front of it left out
  (the recipe's `behind`, §7.3) — the picture the clip reads, not the graded
  one — and centres the ranges on that pixel (hue and saturation on, luma
  re-centred only if on, a level range shifted back inside 0–1, a pixel under
  chroma 0.06 turning hue off), one undo step "Qualifier". A click in the void
  beeps and stays armed; Esc, a second press of the button or a selection
  change ends it.
- **Text** clips edit inline on double-click (an `NSTextView` overlay
  positioned in canvas space, sized after every change to the box the
  typed string will have once applied — it grows and wraps as the text
  will), in the render's font, tracking and line spacing at the canvas's
  scale; an UPPERCASE text is edited as typed (§11 K-3). Return applies
  the text and ⇧Return starts a new line; Esc leaves an unchanged edit
  and asks Cancel / Revert / Apply before dropping a changed one (§11
  K-2). Line and arrow shapes have endpoint handles.
- **Snapping** while dragging: canvas center lines, edges, thirds, and
  other visible clips' edges and centers. Guides draw while snapped;
  ⌘ suppresses. Candidates are computed in Swift from the layer rects;
  the nearest-within-threshold pick is `rc_snap_resolve`.
- The overlay draws in the same coordinate space as the video by mapping
  through the `AVPlayerLayer.videoRect`, so handles and pixels never
  drift apart.
- Canvas drags go through the document's `beginLiveEdit` /
  `updateLiveEdit` / `endLiveEdit` trio (Rasterize's pattern): inspector
  fields update live, the player's composition is not rebuilt until the
  gesture ends (§7.6).
- **Seeing the grade** (grading GR3 and grading-2 G2-1 and G2-4, §11 GR-4,
  GR-5, GR-10, GR-13; hdr H-3, HD-7, HD-14 (g)), four View toggles, view state
  that nothing persists (a window opens with all off):
  **Show Ungraded** ⌃⌘U draws this window's preview — and the Preview Window,
  the same player — as if no clip were graded (the recipe's `ungraded`
  option, §7.3), with a small mono "Ungraded" tag at the pane's top-left in
  the 24 pt void that takes no clicks; an export, Save Frame… and the
  eyedropper never see it. **Show Selection** (no shortcut; also the
  Qualifier section's switch) draws, for ONE adjustment clip, what its
  qualifier selects instead of its grade — opaque grey, white fully selected,
  black untouched, times any matte cutting it — with every layer in front of
  it left out and black where the clip is not on the frame (the recipe's
  `selection`, §7.3), on this window's preview and Preview Window and the
  scopes; a "Selection" tag sits beside "Ungraded" (the two in one row,
  "Selection" in the corner when "Ungraded" is off). Turned on, it binds the
  first selected adjustment clip; a selection change rebinds it to the first
  selected adjustment clip or turns it off when none is selected; it is
  disabled while off with no adjustment clip selected. An export, Save
  Frame…, the look gallery, the eyedropper and `render_frame` never see it.
  **Show SDR Version** (no shortcut, always enabled) draws this window's
  preview as an HLG project's SDR version (§11 HD-4: the recipe option
  `dynamic_range: "sdr"`, §7.3; the composition recoloured BT.709 with no
  rebuild), with an "SDR Version" tag third in the row; in an SDR project no
  pixel changes and the tag still shows. An export, Save Frame…, the
  eyedropper and `render_frame` never see it (`render_frame {sdr_version}`
  asks for the same picture).
  **Scopes** ⌃⌘W puts a fixed 248 pt panel at the
  pane's right edge, between the picture and the inspector, the pane's full
  height, not resizable; the canvas ends at its leading edge (off, zero
  wide), and a modal canvas mode covers the canvas, never the panel. The
  panel holds two slots stacked from its top, each a header — a 120 pt
  picker at the left (Waveform, Parade, Vectorscope, Histogram; the same
  scope may be in both) and the faint mono 9 caption "Rec. 709 · encoded"
  right-aligned — over its scope's body, 224 pt wide inside 12 pt insets. A
  window opens with Waveform over Vectorscope; a pick lasts while the window
  is open, across hide and show, and has no menu item, key or tool. **The
  sizes:** each body has a preferred height — Waveform 200 pt with its two
  readouts, Parade 200 pt, Vectorscope a 224 pt square, Histogram 140 pt
  with the two readouts — and when the pane cannot hold both slots at those
  heights, both bodies shrink by one common factor to fit, the vectorscope
  staying square (its side the smaller of the slot's width and its body's
  height) and centred; below 360 pt of pane height only the top slot shows,
  its body the smaller of its preferred height and the room. **The
  bodies**, each a near-black well in both appearances over the composited
  preview frame's encoded values: the **Waveform**, Rec. 709 luma Y′ as a
  white trace in log density, 256 column bins by 256 levels, graticule
  lines at 0/25/50/75/100 % labelled 0, 50 and 100 in a gutter; red 3 pt
  marks on the well's top edge over each column where ≥ 0.5 % of its pixels
  clip high (level 255) and on its bottom edge for low (level 0); and the
  readouts "Highlights clipped 12.5 %" and "Shadows clipped 0.0 %" for the
  whole frame ("—" before the first frame). The **Parade**, R′, G′ and B′
  each binned as the waveform bins Y′, in three wells 4 pt apart after the
  same labelled gutter, each with the waveform's graticule, its density
  tinted red, green or blue and its own clip marks; no readouts. The
  **Vectorscope**, a square with Rec. 709 Cb running right and Cr up, 256
  bins a side centred on neutral, the counts as a white log density; a
  faint centre cross; the six 75 % targets as 7 pt boxes labelled R, Mg, B,
  Cy, G and Yl in mono 9; and the skin line from the centre to the edge; no
  circles, no readouts. Three 16 pt square cells "1×", "2×" and "4×", sharing
  1 pt borders, sit 4 pt in from the square's bottom-right corner in mono 9,
  the current zoom's filled; a click is that zoom (§11 GR-31): the counts are
  binned at it, `128 + round(255·zoom·c)`, so a near-grey picture spreads
  over 2× or 4× the bins, and at ×2 and ×4 a pixel off the plot is not
  counted. A target off the plot at the zoom (all six from ×2) is a 6 pt rim
  tick from the square's edge inward toward it, labelled inside. The zoom is
  per slot and per window, ×1 for a new window, kept across hide and show
  and a pick of another scope and back, with no menu item, key or default.
  The **Histogram**, levels 0…255 left to
  right, each plane's height the log of its count normalised to the peak:
  R′, G′ and B′ as translucent tinted fills, Y′ a white line on top, and the
  waveform's two readouts, read from Y′. **In an HLG project** (§11 HD-8,
  HD-15) the scopes read the frame's HLG signal: the caption reads "HLG ·
  cd/m²" over a Waveform or Parade and "Rec. 2100 HLG" over a Vectorscope
  or Histogram, following the last trace shown; Y′ takes BT.2020's weights
  and the vectorscope BT.2020's matrix, its targets BT.2111's 75 % bars and
  its skin line the lifted patch's (139.9°); the waveform's and parade's
  gutter labels at 0/25/50/75/100 % read 0, 10, 51, 203 and 1000 (cd/m²,
  right-aligned 2 pt from the well), and the 75 % line, reference white, is
  dashed (3 pt on, 3 pt off, white α 0.45) in place of its solid line; the
  clip marks and readouts are unchanged (level 255 is 1,000 cd/m²). With
  Show SDR Version on, every scope reads the SDR version as an SDR
  project's does ("Rec. 709 · encoded"); Show Ungraded keeps the HLG
  reading. Paused the panel follows every
  edit; playing, it may lead the shown frame by the few frames AVFoundation
  composes ahead. Its GPU passes run only while it is shown, one per scope
  (and vectorscope zoom) its visible slots show.
