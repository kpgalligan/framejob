# 7.9g Agent — Text Behind Subject

The app-state tools are §7.9e; this file holds the one road map "creator-jobs" CO5 added (§11
CO-54), the mirror of Insert ▸ Text Behind Subject (§9).

`text_behind_subject {clip?, at?, content?}` — puts a title behind the person in a video clip.
An app-state composite of ordinary ops with no `scope` key: it runs on the main thread, makes
exactly one undo step, "Text Behind Subject", needs no key (the masks are Vision's, on the Mac;
§11 CJ-3) and leaves the selection as it was. It is the hundred-and-thirteenth product tool.

**What it does** (§11 CO-53). It runs `BehindSubjectPlan`, the planner the menu item runs:

- `at` is ticks, snapped to the frame grid; the playhead when left out.
- Without `clip` it picks among the top-level clips of a video file playing at `at` (start ≤
  `at` < end; `CutoutStatus.canCutOut`; video on) on a track neither hidden nor a matte track:
  the front-most selected one, else the front-most with a cutout, else the front-most.
- The ops: `add_track {index, name: "Behind Subject"}` directly above the clip's track and above
  the unbroken run of matte tracks in front of it (so no matte targets the new track, §11 MT-2:
  with `[V, M1→V, M2]` the index is 3); `add_text_clip` (the Title preset, `content` as its text
  when given) on it at `at` for min(4 s, the clip's end − `at`); `set_video {behind_subject:
  true}` on the title; and, only when the clip has no cutout, `set_video {cutout: {background:
  "keep"}}` on it, so the camera stays whole and its person still hides the title.

**The answer.** `{ok, clip, track, text, duration, cutout_added, project}`: the clip the title
is behind, the new track's id, the title's clip id, its duration in ticks, whether the clip was
given a cutout, and the usual project summary.

**Refused, nothing done**, each in its own sentence: a nest open ("text_behind_subject works on
the top-level timeline: set_scope with no nest first."); an empty `content` ("content must not
be empty"); no candidate at `at` ("No clip of a video file plays at T on a shown track that is
not a matte track, so there is no one to put the title behind."); a named clip that cannot hold
one ("Clip N cannot have a title put behind its subject: it must be a clip of a video file on the
timeline, on a shown track that is not a matte track."); and a named clip that does not play at
`at` ("Clip N does not play at T: pass an at inside it.").

Any drawing clip but an adjustment or nest clip can be put behind the subject by hand with
`set_video {behind_subject: true}` (§5.2, §6); the relation and how it is drawn are §7.3's
Subjects and §11 CO-50, CO-51.
