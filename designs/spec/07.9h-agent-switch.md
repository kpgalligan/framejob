# 7.9h Agent — copy pieces and Switch Angle

The app-state tools are §7.9e; this file holds the two road map "creator-jobs" SW1 added (§11
SW-11…SW-15): the op tool `copy_pieces` and `switch_angle`, the mirror of Clip ▸ Switch Angle ▸
(§9). Neither sends anything anywhere, so neither needs a key (§11 CJ-3). `copy_pieces` is the
hundred-and-twentieth product tool and the sixty-ninth op; `switch_angle` the
hundred-and-twenty-first.

**`copy_pieces {clip, track, ranges, video?, audio?}`** — an op tool, printed from the core's
table (§6b), so it takes `scope`. One undo step, "Copy Pieces", committed through `editResult`,
whose answer lists the new clips. `ranges` is read by `rangesArg` (each end a time in ticks,
snapped; a lone `[from, to]` taken as one range). A missing `ranges` and an empty list are
refused by the tool ("ranges is empty — name at least one [from, to] pair."), where the core
reads an empty list as an identity; `AgentCatalog+Declarations.swift` declares it
(`minItems: 1`). Every other refusal is the core's sentence (§6b).

**`switch_angle {clip, angle}`** — re-picks one shot from another camera angle. Both arguments
are required ids: `clip` the shot, `angle` a track. It takes no `scope` key: it reads the
container `set_scope` has open, as `text_behind_subject` does, and runs on the main thread. It
runs `SwitchAngle.plan`, the planner the menu runs, on that container's handle and commits its
ops through `performGroupedEdit`, one undo step.

- **Covering** (§11 SW-13, SW-15 (d)). A track covers the shot when its media clips with a
  picture leave no tick of the shot's span uncovered; a clip with its picture off, or on a
  hidden track, still counts.
- **The sequence** (§11 SW-14, SW-15 (e)). `delete` the shot; `trim_end` the clip before it
  (ending after its start) to its start and `trim_start` the clip after it (starting before its
  end) to its end, each `alone`; one `copy_pieces` per angle clip the span crosses, each taking
  `[max(start, s), min(end, the next one's start, e))` with the shot's `video`/`audio`
  switches; the two neighbours trimmed back to their old edges; `set_transition` and
  `set_transition_style` giving the first piece the shot's `transition_in` and the clip after
  its own. A punch-in, grade or action on the old shot does not carry.
- **Tried one at a time.** Each op is tried on the handle the last one left, because
  `ReelProject.applying([ReelOp])` does not stop at a refusal. A refusal before the trim-backs,
  or a trim away held short of its target (a ramp's held front edge), refuses the whole plan
  and nothing is committed. A trim-back that is refused or held short is left out together with
  its edge's transition ops (the edge's clip loses a `transition_in` it still holds), and the
  step is "Switch Angle (transition removed)".

**The answer**, in this key order: `{ok, step, pieces, transitions_removed, project}` — the
step's name, the new pieces' ids in time order, how many of the two transitions were dropped
(0–2), and the usual project summary.

**Refused, nothing done**, each in its own sentence: the angle is the shot's own track ("track T
is clip C's own track; an angle is another track"); the shot's track is locked ("track T is
locked, so clip C cannot be re-picked"); a linked shot ("clip C is linked to clip P (Detach
Audio's pair); Switch Angle re-picks a clip with no link"); the angle does not cover the shot
("track T does not cover clip C: nothing with a picture plays on it at tick X"); a step of the
sequence refused (that op's own sentence); and a held trim ("clip X's edge is held at tick Y,
short of tick Z, so clip B cannot be re-picked"). An unknown clip or track is "no clip with id
N" / "no track with id N".
