# 7.9h Agent — copy pieces, Switch Angle and Switch by Speaker

The app-state tools are §7.9e; this file holds the two road map "creator-jobs" SW1 added (§11
SW-11…SW-15): the op tool `copy_pieces` and `switch_angle`, the mirror of Clip ▸ Switch Angle ▸
(§9); and the one SW2b added (§11 SW-23, SW-26): `switch_by_speaker`, the mirror of Clip ▸
Switch by Speaker… (§9). None sends anything anywhere, so none needs a key (§11 CJ-3).
`copy_pieces` is the hundred-and-twentieth product tool and the sixty-ninth op; `switch_angle`
the hundred-and-twenty-first; `switch_by_speaker` the hundred-and-twenty-third.

**`copy_pieces {clip, track, ranges, video?, audio?}`** — an op tool, printed from the core's
table (§6b), so it takes `scope`. One undo step, "Copy Pieces", committed through `editResult`,
whose answer lists the new clips. `ranges` is read by `rangesArg` (each end a time in ticks,
snapped; a lone `[from, to]` taken as one range). A missing `ranges` and an empty list are
refused by the tool ("ranges is empty — name at least one [from, to] pair."), where the core
reads an empty list as an identity; `AgentCatalog+Declarations.swift` declares it
(`minItems: 1`). Every other refusal is the core's sentence (§6b).

**`switch_angle {clip, angle}`** — re-picks one shot from another camera angle. Both arguments
are required ids: `clip` the shot, `angle` a track. It takes no `scope` key: it reads the
container `set_scope` has open, as `text_behind_subject` does, and runs on the main thread. It
runs `SwitchAngle.plan`, the planner the menu runs, on that container's handle and commits its
ops through `performGroupedEdit`, one undo step.

- **Covering** (§11 SW-13, SW-15 (d)). A track covers the shot when its media clips with a
  picture leave no tick of the shot's span uncovered; a clip with its picture off, or on a
  hidden track, still counts.
- **The sequence** (§11 SW-14, SW-15 (e)). `delete` the shot; `trim_end` the clip before it
  (ending after its start) to its start and `trim_start` the clip after it (starting before its
  end) to its end, each `alone`; one `copy_pieces` per angle clip the span crosses, each taking
  `[max(start, s), min(end, the next one's start, e))` with the shot's `video`/`audio`
  switches; the two neighbours trimmed back to their old edges; `set_transition` and
  `set_transition_style` giving the first piece the shot's `transition_in` and the clip after
  its own. A punch-in, grade or action on the old shot does not carry.
- **Tried one at a time.** Each op is tried on the handle the last one left, because
  `ReelProject.applying([ReelOp])` does not stop at a refusal. A refusal before the trim-backs,
  or a trim away held short of its target (a ramp's held front edge), refuses the whole plan
  and nothing is committed. A trim-back that is refused or held short is left out together with
  its edge's transition ops (the edge's clip loses a `transition_in` it still holds), and the
  step is "Switch Angle (transition removed)".

**The answer**, in this key order: `{ok, step, pieces, transitions_removed, project}` — the
step's name, the new pieces' ids in time order, how many of the two transitions were dropped
(0–2), and the usual project summary.

**Refused, nothing done**, each in its own sentence: the angle is the shot's own track ("track T
is clip C's own track; an angle is another track"); the shot's track is locked ("track T is
locked, so clip C cannot be re-picked"); a linked shot ("clip C is linked to clip P (Detach
Audio's pair); Switch Angle re-picks a clip with no link"); the angle does not cover the shot
("track T does not cover clip C: nothing with a picture plays on it at tick X"); a step of the
sequence refused (that op's own sentence); and a held trim ("clip X's edge is held at tick Y,
short of tick Z, so clip B cannot be re-picked"). An unknown clip or track is "no clip with id
N" / "no track with id N".

**`switch_by_speaker {speakers?, wide?, from?, to?, min_shot?, min_turn?, lead?, crosstalk?,
bleed_db?, dominance_db?, wide_after?, mute_cameras?, track?, apply?}`** — cuts a Program track
from the selected cameras, showing whoever talks on each microphone (§11 SW-23, SW-26). It takes
no `scope` key: it reads the container `set_scope` has open and the document's selection, runs on
the server thread with its reads and edit in main hops (§11 SW-86, `creatorOffMainTools`), and
runs the menu's own `SpeakerSwitch.read`, `plan` (§11 SW-20…SW-24) and `edit`.

- **The faces mode** (§11 SW-80…SW-84): with one microphone shared by two or more angles — the
  default pairing when the selection holds exactly one microphone, every angle with it and no
  wide — who talks is decided from each face speaker's cached `faces` (the largest face's mouth
  gap over width, its population std over 0.5 s centred, the top angle at ≥ 1.5× the next and
  above 0.02 while the microphone's v ≥ 0.5, else crosstalk) and SW-22 runs unchanged over those
  labels. A named `wide` without `speakers` drops a speaker on it who shares a microphone
  (`withoutWide`), so one microphone, angles A and B and `wide` B is `[(M, A)]` in the
  microphones mode. `min_shot` absent is 3 s in the faces mode.
- **The wait**: one main hop reads the arguments, `read` and the faces the store misses
  (`facesWanted`: each face-speaker clip's part in the range ± 0.25 s, misses merged per media);
  with none it plans and edits there. Otherwise it wants them under an owner of its own and waits
  at most 60 s (`AnalysisLane.callWaitLimit`), then a last hop refuses "the project changed while
  Switch by Speaker was analyzing, so nothing was switched. Call switch_by_speaker again." or
  reads again and runs the sequence below. Past the wait: "The faces analysis of media N did not
  finish in the 60 s a call waits; what it covered is kept — call switch_by_speaker again to
  continue."; a failed job "The faces analysis of media N failed: why."; Stop "The faces analysis
  of media N was stopped before it finished, so nothing was switched."

- **Arguments**, every one optional. `speakers` `[{mic, angle}]`, track ids, both required in
  each item and no other key (a camera's detached sound may be a `mic`); default SW-20's pairing
  over the selection. `wide` an angle's track id, or null for none; absent, the one angle no
  speaker uses when exactly one is left. `from` and `to` ticks, both or neither, each snapped to
  the frame grid, `to` after `from`; default the In–Out range, else the selected angle clips'
  extent. `min_shot` 0.5–30 s (2), `min_turn` 0.2–10 s (1), `lead` 0–1 s (0.2), `bleed_db` 3–40
  (15), `dominance_db` 0–30 (8), `wide_after` 2–600 s or null (null, off), `crosstalk` `"wide"`
  or `"hold"` (`"wide"`), `mute_cameras` (on when no microphone is an angle), `track` (Replace the
  shots on that track), `apply` (true). The sheet takes the same values and never sets `min_turn`
  or `dominance_db`.
- **Errors**, before anything is read: a number outside its range ("min_shot is 0.1 s; it runs
  0.5–30 s."), `crosstalk` neither word, `from` without `to`, `to` not after `from`, a malformed
  speaker, an unknown track ("no track with id N"), and a `wide` that is not an angle ("wide:
  track T is not an angle"). An unreadable plan is "The project's audio plan could not be read…".
- **Refusals**, nothing changed, each after "Switch by Speaker was refused: ": `track`'s first
  failed test (§11 SW-23 (e): "Track T cannot take the shots: it is locked." · "…: it holds a
  selected clip." · "…: it is not in front of the angles." · "…: it has nothing between A and B
  to replace." · "…: clip C is not a piece of an angle."); then the plan's refusals, every one,
  joined by spaces (§11 SW-24 as SW-83 and SW-84 amend it: the shared-microphone refusal only
  in the microphones mode; a camera still being analysed after the microphones' problems; a camera
  with no readable mouth after the no-speech refusal; the clip ceiling last and alone); then a
  linked angle clip crossing
  the range ("clip C on track T is linked to clip P (Detach Audio's pair); Switch by Speaker cuts
  between cameras with no link — Attach Audio first"); then an op of the edit the core refuses
  ("Switch by Speaker was refused at <op>: <reason>").
- **The ops** (§11 SW-23), tried one at a time on the container's handle: `add_track {index: the
  front-most angle's + 1, name: "Program"}`, or with `track` a `split` of each of its clips
  straddling `from`, then `to`, and one `delete` of its clips inside the range; one `copy_pieces`
  per angle clip a shot crosses (`video: true, audio: false`), in the order of its first range;
  per angle track, front first, `set_track {hidden: true}` when every clip on it is a picture clip
  inside the range, else a `split` of each picture clip straddling `from`, then `to`, and
  `set_enabled {video: false}` on each picture clip inside; with `mute_cameras`, `audio: false` on
  each such clip whose track is not a microphone. A microphone-only track is never edited. The
  selection after is the one before plus every new half a split made of a selected clip.
- **The answer**: `{ok, applied, step, track, shots, ops, added, speakers, wide, mute_cameras,
  mode, note, project}` — `mode` `"microphones"` or `"faces"`, `note` "Faces guess who is talking;
  check the cuts." in the faces mode only; `step` "Switch by Speaker" only when applied; `track` the Program track's id (null
  when not applied and no `track` named); `shots` `[{angle, start, end}]` in time order; `ops` the
  edit's op count; `added` the clips SW-24's ceiling counts; the pairing used; whether the
  cameras were muted; the project summary. `apply: false` answers the same and edits nothing.
