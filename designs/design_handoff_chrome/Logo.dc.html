Reelcut · identity
The cut mark
Two clips overlap; the playhead stands in the overlap. The shape is the edit — where two pieces of footage meet and where you are in them. Same grammar as the Rasterize mark: flat geometry, multiplied inks, one paper ground, one ink rule.
How it is built
A 120 × 120 box. Two 54 × 52 rounded rectangles, radius 8, at y 36 — forest starting at x 18, butter at x 48. They overlap by 24, and that overlap is drawn as its own rounded rectangle in the multiplied ink, so the mark survives any renderer that ignores blend modes. The playhead is 7 wide on the overlap's centre line, x 56.5, running y 20 to 102, with a 21-wide head whose apex meets the clips' top edge.
