## Context

The owner's goal, from the survey's question of 2026-10-30, was to turn their RØDE USB desktop-microphone recording, `samples/Rode-usb-mic.m4a` (30.4 s, AAC mono, 48 kHz), into usable audio for videos, and to know what a professional-grade editor's audio toolset holds. It peaks at −18.6 dBFS, integrates to −44.7 LUFS (−41.67 LUFS as exported, the mono file copied into both channels), and 65 % of its 50 ms windows lie within 6 dB of its −64.3 dBFS floor (the pauses). Frame Job's audio was a creator-tier strip: peak Normalize and the export's loudness target both stopped near −27 LUFS, because neither could reduce a peak, and the compressor (threshold −18 dB, before the fader) never acted on this file. The survey read seven editors' own documentation and tiered the gaps: A (professional-grade), B (creator and screencast), C (nice to have). On 2026-10-30 the owner answered the road map's five choices — "Yes, lift scopes, and allow Apple's built-in audio effects", then "OK" to each of the rest — and added "We'll address other questions as we progress. If builds can be run in multiple lanes, do so." The road map took tiers A and B; tier C stayed out of it. (orig "Goal", "What it delivers first", "The owner's choices"; survey §2, §5)

## Decisions

- **Live stages are Frame Job's own DSP; Apple's units render offline (AU-3, S1).** S1's rule, fixed before its runs: a live stage allocates and locks nothing on the tap's thread, reads byte-identically twice and at any buffer size, sits within 0.1 dB of its analytic response, has one constant latency ≤ 512 samples, and eight stereo stages cost ≤ 1 % of real time. Frame Job's own stages passed all five; no Apple unit passed for any stage: their output depends on how the stream is cut into render calls (up to −34 dBFS for `AUDynamicsProcessor` on irregular slices, which the tap's cuts and the player's 4,096-frame buffers make), `AUPeakLimiter` limits sample peak at a fixed 0 dBFS (+3.10 dBTP on a crest signal), and `AUNBandEQ` was only 1.5–1.6× cheaper. So AU-3 stood unamended. S1 also fixed the live stages' coding rules: scalar per-sample code or element-wise vDSP only (`vDSP_biquad` and `vDSP_conv` give different bits for different buffer sizes), a stage's `reset()` restores its fresh state, and the tap keeps resetting at StartOfStream (the player's silent pre-roll otherwise leaves a gate closed). (orig AP0 "S1")
- **One fixed chain, each stage off and inert by default (AU-3, AU-54, AU-143, AU-66 (a)).** The clip's chain is isolation → voice effect → channels → high-pass/EQ → hum → noise reduction → gate → leveller → de-esser → compressor → limiter → fader; then the track's volume and pan, the track's compressor and limiter on its sub-mix (AU-151), and the export's master (AU-27). Isolation moved ahead of the live chain because it renders offline from the media (S2), and the voice effect renders from isolation's crossfade. A project using none of the clip and track stages plays as before and writes no new key (each is written only when set); its export now always passes the master, which leaves a mix under the ceiling sample-identical (AP2b's X6). (orig AP0 "What it changes in the phases", AP5, AP14, AP6b)
- **Loudness is the export's figure, from a 10 ms cache (AU-10…AU-17, S4).** 100 ms blocks missed the export's own meter by up to −0.139 LU over random windows; 10 ms blocks stayed within 0.076 (97.5 % of 200 windows within 0.1 LU), and 10 ms is the peaks grid, so the waveform's window, the reversed mirror and the source map apply unchanged. The export copies a mono clip into both channels (+3.01 LU), so every reading counts mono twice: the owner's recording is −41.67 LUFS where its file reads −44.7. AU-4's gated target stands; speech-only loudness read 1.1–1.7 LU higher. (orig AP0 "S4", AP1)
- **The clip limiter is told the base volume and leads its own delay (AU-20…AU-26).** The fader is downstream of the tap, so the stage is told the clip's base volume, fades and ducks only ever lowering it (AU-22). It runs for the whole composition track once any clip asks, so the track's delay never changes at a cut (158 samples, led out by 1,975 ticks). Its detector takes the maximum over a 64-tap Kaiser (β 10) detector's phases and BS.1770 Annex 2's: a detector the size of Annex 2's alone let broadband onsets through by up to +0.97 dB, and Annex 2 over-reads speech by 0.14 dB, so a limiter exact by an accurate meter alone would read −0.86 dBTP on the export's meter. Two departures from S1's code, neither from its arithmetic: the FIR taps are element-wise vDSP, because scalar loops missed the real-time deadline in the Debug app's `-Onone` build, and the release envelope is a Double, because a Float one-pole stalls near 1. (orig AP0 "S1", AP2)
- **The export always runs a float master (AU-27…AU-29, #86).** S3 found two clips, each under its own limiter, reaching +1.49 dBTP through a crossfade, so a master limiter at `peak_dbtp` (−1 dBTP by default) runs on every export with sound, with no off switch; the loudness target now limits instead of capping, the gain solved through the limiter's model over 10 ms steps and capped at +40 dB. The mix is decoded as float (#86 closed) and written at 16 or 24 bits on WAV and AIFF, rounded once, never dithered. (orig AP2b)
- **Isolation is Apple's voice model, rendered offline and crossfaded (AU-50…AU-57, S2).** `AUSoundIsolation` with parameter 1 = 0 selects High Quality Voice (the unit's default, Voice, loses everything above 10.5 kHz). Each channel renders as its own mono render, the constant 4,440-frame latency pinned and discarded, 4 s of the media fed first. The unit's 50 % equals a sample-aligned 50/50 mix of dry and a 100 % render, so Amount is a crossfade of one cached render and is not in the cache key. Mid-file windows differ by about −51 dB from a whole-file render, so proofs compare levels and alignment, never bytes. De-reverb (survey B4) rides on it: C50 +5.2 dB and the late tail −5.3 dB on a synthetic RT60 0.6 s room, so the section says it "reduces room echo" and there is no separate control (AU-56). (orig AP0 "S2", AP5)
- **EQ is RBJ biquads in Double at 17 fixed positions (AU-30…AU-39).** Seven named slots and three presets, each preset writing only its own slot. The hum comb's Q is 10·k / A (A = 10^(−depth/40)): the plain Q = 10·k comb missed −depth once cascaded and cut the voice between harmonics, the adversary's objection, taken before the code. (orig AP3)
- **The gate's threshold is relative to the clip's loudness; the de-esser never boosts (AU-40…AU-49).** A level fixed in dBFS was what failed on this microphone (survey §2), so the gate reads the clip's own measured L, and an export waits for it. The draft de-esser `x − (1 − g)·band` boosted the sibilant band (+2.6 dB at 6 kHz for a 6 dB range, a 4th-order high-pass being 180° out at its cut), so the reduction goes through an LR4 crossover, `low + g·band`, and the Butterworth stays the sidechain only. (orig AP4)
- **Enhance Voice is a composite that writes ordinary settings, its volume solved on the processed sound (AU-60…AU-65).** It measures the clip and writes high-pass, isolation or noise reduction, gate, de-esser when the high band is hot, a compressor set from the clip's own level, the limiter and the volume, as one undo step; a second press changes nothing. The first attempt solved the volume through the compressor's block-rate model and exported the owner's recording at −13.95 LUFS for a −16 target: cleaning the pauses changes which blocks BS.1770's relative gate counts (the gate moved the export +2.4 LU, isolation +2.6 LU, the EQ −0.7 LU). On 2026-10-31 the owner chose (A), measure the processed sound, for Enhance and Loudness Normalize alike (AU-64): the clip rendered through its own chain under the Measuring Sound sheet, verified to ±0.1 LU in at most three renders, its base volume carried as one constant ramp because a gated clip under a limiter read 0.19–0.25 LU high without it. (orig AP6 "Asked", "Deviations")
- **The leveller is a curve made ahead of time, applied with no state (AU-66, AU-67).** The first build, a slow stateful rider, could not meet its own acceptance row: its hold carried each segment's gain through a pause into the next, which started 12 dB off, so the off-mic fixture's range went 13.63 LU in, 13.96 out. On 2026-10-31 the owner chose look ahead: a gain curve per phrase from the clip's loudness and speech, so each phrase starts at its level (13.63 → 2.32 LU), and the per-phrase start held to ±3 dB (the owner's choice on the same measurement; the material's sentences open louder than their middles). Enhance turns it on above 6 LU of the speech's short-term range; the owner's recording reads 4.610 LU, so it stays off there. The owner also ordered the processed solve's non-repeat "fixed, not filed"; measured, it did not reproduce (AU-64 (i)). (orig AP6b "Asked", "The answer", "Step 3", "P2 (b)")
- **Enhance at landing is a follow-up, its own undo step (AU-68, AU-69).** The landing commits at once and is unchanged; Enhance follows once the voice is analysed, as "Enhance Voice", from the recorder's "Enhance the microphone when it lands" box or `record_screen`'s `enhance`. (orig AP6c)
- **The live meter reads the true mix, rebuilt from the taps (AU-70…AU-78, S3).** Nothing in the macOS 26.2 SDK reaches an `AVPlayer`'s mixed audio; summing the taps by timestamp, each times the plan's envelope, matched the export's mix within 0.0067 LU momentary, where the old sum of per-track peaks over-read the true peak by up to 3.85 dB. It measures at ×1 only and is pre-master (the preview has none). The player hands each track 4,096-frame buffers and, after a seek, one or two buffers of the OLD position, so the reset rule asks only of a chunk that moves its track (AU-72 amended in the lane). (orig AP0 "S3", AP7)
- **Every audio parameter animates through actions, not keyframes (AU-80…AU-89).** The owner chose (a), actions, on 2026-10-30 (AU-80), keeping spec §2's "Actions, not keyframes": the volume line on each clip edits the level in force, and an ⌥-press adds a point as an action (or a split of one). The plan carries each audio segment's `automation` (the core's evaluation cut to the twelve tap keys), and the tap reads it on an absolute 32-frame grid, so a render is the same at any buffer size; switches change at an action's end. (orig AP8, AP8b)
- **Split edits are a linked pair, following in time, not in track (AU-90…AU-97).** The owner chose (a), the linked pair, on 2026-10-30, with the half you grab deciding (⌥ on an edge was already Rate Stretch). A partner follows in time but keeps its own track, so a detached sound can reach a track of its own and a J-cut between detached shots never becomes a dissolve; Attach Audio merges a pair back. (orig AP9 "Decided")
- **Tracks: linear stages exact everywhere, dynamics exact only in the export (AU-100…AU-109, AU-150…AU-156).** S3 measured track gain, pan and EQ per composition track exact (−145 dB) and dynamics not (a limiter +0.85 dB over its ceiling through a crossfade, a nest bus +1.4–1.8 dB); one reader with one mix output per timeline track sums to the mix within −147.7 dB. On 2026-10-30 the owner chose (1 (b)) a preview that stays approximate for track dynamics and says so, and (2 (a)) stems as separate files. So the export reads per-track sub-mixes in lockstep, each through its track's compressor and limiter, and the preview runs them per composition track, which the meter flags (`tracks_approximate`). An exact preview through `AVAudioEngine` (0.02 ppm of drift, 60–150 ms to start) would rewrite the transport, so it is a road-map candidate (#391). Stems are pre-master: no loudness gain, no limiter. (orig AP0 "S3", AP10, AP10b, AP10c)
- **A speech duck reads spans saved on the media (AU-110…AU-116).** The owner chose (a), saved in the project, on 2026-10-30, so the plan stays a pure function of the project: `set_media_speech` writes the VAD's spans in the same undo step that turns Speech on (the owner's recording: 10 spans, 188 bytes). Fades and crossfades take four shapes; a crossfade's default stays R5-2's constant power. (orig AP11)
- **Match Audio is a composite over the volume, the limiter and the EQ's five tonal slots (AU-120…AU-129).** Match Loudness is Loudness Normalize to the reference's figure; Match EQ fits speech-only third-octave spectra measured on renders. Refining the shelves first broke one fixture and not refining them another, so the refined shelves are taken only when they leave no weighted band at the bell threshold (AU-126). (orig AP12 "Deviations")
- **Scrub audio is grains of the player's mix (AU-130…AU-136).** 80 ms with 5 ms sin² edges, forward from the frame-snapped playhead, one ahead and only on a new frame, read from a composition built only for scrubbing and played through an `AVAudioEngine` only scrubbing uses; no toggle, key or tool. Each grain's stateful stages start fresh, because `AudioTapContext.prepare` makes a new chain per reader. (orig AP13)
- **Voice effects are E8's fixed chains of Apple units, rendered offline (AU-140…AU-149, #123).** Sixteen kinds, each TimePitch → EQ → Distortion → Delay → Reverb scaled by an amount, with a measured makeup per kind and the chain's latency discarded. (orig AP14)
- **The working arrangement.** Lanes were split by the files they touch; a phase whose prerequisite was in another lane waited for it to land. A format bump was provisional until its phase landed, so the integration renumbered formats (AP2's lane wrote 28, AP5's 30, AP3's 31, AP14's 33, AP6b's 36), and manual-check numbers likewise. AP2, AP6, AP8 and AP10 were each built in parts. (orig "The orchestrator's defaults", each record's "Integrated")

## Known limits

- **The preview has no master and approximates track dynamics** (AU-73, AU-100, AU-153): the meter reads the mix before the export's loudness gain and master limiter and says so, and flags a track compressor or limiter as approximate. An exact preview is #391.
- **Isolation and voice effects** played dry on a time-scaled or ramped segment until #387 lifted that (2026-11-10, AU-55 and AU-145 as amended: the processed window is what gets retimed; on such a clip the Amount is in a file, so a new one renders before it is heard). They render the window again after a trim, and a split take's windows start cold (S2: a seam about −30…−50 dB). Waveforms, Normalize's reading and Remove Silences read the dry media (AU-148).
- **The voice effects' character is a person's** (SE-32): K7 (telephone) reads +3.3 dB at its bell's 3 kHz octave and +2.7 dB at 4 kHz, under the sketch's +3; a larger bell would be the owner's change to AU-141.
- **Loudness figures carry their meters' small disagreements**: the limiter's model against its stage 0.090 LU (AP2 L8); Annex 2's filter ripple +0.204 dB at fs/4 60° (AP7); AU-64's verify render needed the base volume as one constant ramp, and why the ramp-free render read 0.19–0.25 LU high on a gated, limited clip was not found.
- **The live meter** resets on a Bluetooth output (#389); it measures only at ×1. It counted the player's silent filler after a seek as the media there until #405 (2026-11-11, AU-161: a filler is a mark with no samples, so a seek's measurement begins at its first audio, 3,072 frames past the seek as measured, and pause and continue no longer resets after a pause of a few seconds); the frames between the sum and the first audio after a pause read as silence.
- **Scrub audio** (AU-131…AU-134): a grain that starts inside a gain ramp differs from the export's read at the same frames, because AVFoundation evaluates a volume ramp from where its reader starts (the player seeking into a ramp is in the same position); the silence after a hold is proved on the scheduler's clock, since `ScrubAudio` never reads a grain's `at`; a queue lagging a rebuild, or a read longer than 80 ms, plays its grains late (PB-14 hears it). These three were accepted at AP13's review.
- **Split edits** (AU-92): under an overwritten picture the linked sound becomes three abutting pieces, the middle one unlinked; `ripple_delete_range` over a J-cut's lead cuts the lead rather than refusing.
- **Synthetic fixtures for everything but the one recording**: the survey's §6 list of further samples (clipping, hum, sibilance, a room, plosives, two mics, a music bed, off-mic, one channel, system audio, camera audio, Spatial Audio, ten minutes) was not recorded; each phase used a fixture with a known answer instead, and the person checks carry the rest.
