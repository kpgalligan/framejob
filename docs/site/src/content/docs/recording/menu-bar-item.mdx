While a recording runs, Frame Job puts an item in the menu bar. For a display or a window the panel is off screen, so this item is the recording's control, wherever you're working.

## What it shows

The item appears when the recording starts, and shows the elapsed time as `m:ss`, then `h:mm:ss` past an hour. Hover over it and its tooltip says which state it's in.

| State | The menu bar shows | Tooltip | Its menu |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recording | A coral ● and the running time | "Frame Job is recording" | **Pause Recording**, **Stop Recording** |
| Paused | ❚❚ and the time, held still | "Frame Job's recording is paused" | **Resume Recording**, **Stop Recording** |

It needs no permission. It appears for an agent's recording too, so you can [pause or stop one](/recording/pause-and-stop/#stop-an-agents-recording). It comes down the moment the recording ends, before the recording has landed.

## Pause, resume and stop

Click the item for its menu.

- **Pause Recording** pauses. The row then reads **Resume Recording**.
- **Stop Recording** stops the recording, running or paused, and adds it to your project.

The time keeps ticking while the menu is open, so you can read it before you decide. [Start, pause and stop](/recording/pause-and-stop/) has the other places to do this. If you choose **Quit Frame Job** while the panel is hidden for a screen recording, Frame Job opens this menu instead of quitting.

## The keyboard mark

While a recording [records shortcuts](/recording/record-shortcuts/), a ⌨︎ mark follows the ● or ❚❚, and the menu starts with a greyed-out **Recording keyboard shortcuts** row. Both show whether the recording is running or paused, and they hold for a recording an agent started with shortcuts on.

## A fixed width

The item is sized once, so the other items in your menu bar don't shift as the seconds tick, when the hours appear, or at a pause. It's sized for times up to 9:59:59, and a little wider when it shows the keyboard mark. Past 9:59:59 the label is wider than that.

## If macOS hides the item

macOS can drop the item from a crowded menu bar or behind the notch. **File ▸ Stop Recording** and **File ▸ Pause Recording** still work once you switch to Frame Job.
