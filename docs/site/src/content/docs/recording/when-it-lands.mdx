The boxes on the recorder panel run the clean-up you'd otherwise do by hand. The recording [lands](/recording/where-it-lands/) exactly as it would without them, and then each ticked box runs its command on what landed, each as its own undo step.

## Choose what runs

Tick the boxes on the panel before you press Record.

| Box | Runs | On | Default | Available when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Remove the camera's background when it lands** | **Clip ▸ Remove Background** | The camera's clips | Off | **Record the camera too** is ticked beside a display or a window |
| **Enhance the microphone when it lands** | **Clip ▸ Enhance Voice** | The microphone's clips | Off | A microphone is chosen, on any source |
| **Zoom in on clicks** | **Clip ▸ Auto Zoom…** | The screen clips | On | The source is a display or a window |
| **Show shortcuts** | **Clip ▸ Add Shortcut Overlays** | The screen clips | On | The source is a display or a window, with **Record shortcuts** ticked |
| **Speed up typing** | **Clip ▸ Speed Up Typing…** | The screen clips | Off | The source is a display or a window, with **Record shortcuts** ticked |

A greyed box keeps its value for next time, and every box is greyed while a recording is under way. The panel remembers all five between uses.

The screen clips are the clips of each stretch's movie that has a [cursor file](/recording/cursor-files/). No other clip is touched, and a voice-over or a camera on its own has none.

:::note
**Zoom in on clicks** is on from the start, so a display or window recording lands with zooms unless you untick it. **Show shortcuts** is on too, but it does nothing until you tick [Record shortcuts](/recording/record-shortcuts/), which is off.
:::

## The order they run in

The steps always run in this order, whichever you tick:

1. **Remove Background**, straight after the landing.
2. **Enhance Voice**, once the sound has been analysed.
3. **Auto Zoom**.
4. **Add Shortcut Overlays**.
5. **Speed Up Typing**, once the speech has been detected.

Each is its own undo step, named as in this list, and they all come after the landing's. **Edit ▸ Undo** takes them off one at a time, last first, and keeps the recording.

The order matters. Zooms are planned on whole clips before the speed-up splits them, and the speed-up leaves each overlay at its pace.

Each command uses the settings its own menu command would, and only Enhance Voice shows a sheet. Nothing asks you a question.

## Remove the camera's background

**Remove the camera's background when it lands** runs [Remove Background](/effects/remove-background/) on the camera's clips straight after the landing, with nothing to wait for. If no camera clip landed, it does nothing and says nothing. [Record a camera beside the screen](/recording/camera-beside-the-screen/#remove-the-cameras-background) has the rest.

## Enhance the microphone

**Enhance the microphone when it lands** runs [Enhance Voice](/audio/enhance-voice/) on each stretch's microphone file, or on a voice-over's files. It never touches the picture, a camera or the system audio.

Enhance Voice needs the sound's loudness and speech analysed first, so it waits for that after the files land. It then runs under the **Measuring Sound** sheet, at the target loudness of the [Normalize](/audio/normalize/) row, and its settings appear in the clip's [Sound tab](/audio/sound-tab/).

Choosing **Cancel** on the sheet leaves the clips as they landed.

**Edit ▸ Undo Enhance Voice** takes the processing off and keeps the recording. The next undo is **Remove Background** if that ran, then the landing's own step: **Add Recording** in an open project, and in a new project a step such as **Add Microphone**, or nothing when the recording is a single file.

If a second recording lands in the same project while the first is still waiting, its clips join the wait and the wait starts again.

## Zoom in on clicks

**Zoom in on clicks** runs [Auto Zoom](/recording/auto-zoom/) with the values the Auto Zoom sheet last used. **Zoom on typing** is turned on when the recording has typing, and off when it hasn't.

## Show shortcuts

**Show shortcuts** runs [Add Shortcut Overlays](/recording/shortcut-overlays/) with its usual settings: at the bottom of the picture, in the dark theme.

## Speed up typing

**Speed up typing** runs [Speed Up Typing](/recording/speed-up-typing/) with the values its sheet last used. It waits until the speech has been detected, so typing you talk over keeps its pace.

A speed-up that would leave a clip silent above ×12 is never applied here, because the panel can't ask you first. The foot of the inspector says so instead. Choose **Clip ▸ Speed Up Typing…** yourself to be asked. See [Sound at high speed](/speed/sound-at-high-speed/).

## What the steps wait for

Enhance Voice, Auto Zoom, Add Shortcut Overlays and Speed Up Typing each hold off while:

- you're dragging in the project
- a sheet is up on its window
- you're inside a different nest from the one the recording landed in

They carry on by themselves once that's over. Each waits for up to 5 minutes. The clock starts at the landing or when the step before it ran. For the first step after Enhance Voice, it starts when Enhance Voice stops waiting.

## When a step can't run

A problem shows at the foot of the [inspector](/getting-started/inspector/), with no beep and no sheet. A step with nothing to do says nothing at all: a recording with no cursor file, a recording with no microphone clip, or no click, shortcut or typing stretch long enough changes nothing and shows no message.

| The inspector says | What happened | What runs next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Enhance Voice did not run on the recording: its microphone was not ready to measure within 5 minutes. Select its clip and choose Clip ▸ Enhance Voice." | The sound wasn't analysed in time, or the project stayed busy. | The other steps |
| "Nothing could be enhanced. …" | There was nothing to measure, for example a silent microphone. The sentence gives the reason. | The other steps |
| "… did not run on the recording: the project was busy for 5 minutes. Select its clips and choose Clip ▸ …" | You were dragging, a sheet was up, or you were in another nest for the whole wait. | Nothing. Run the rest yourself. |
| "Speed Up Typing did not run on the recording: its speech was not detected within 5 minutes. Select its clips and choose Clip ▸ Speed Up Typing…." | Speech detection didn't finish in time, or the project stayed busy. | It's the last step. |
| "Clip … now plays above ×12, so its audio is left out: it is silent in the preview and the export." | The speed-up would take that clip past ×12, so Frame Job didn't make it. Nothing changed: the clip, which can be on another track, keeps its speed and its sound. Choose **Clip ▸ Speed Up Typing…** to be asked. | It's the last step. |
| Any other sentence | The command wouldn't run. This is the sentence it would show from its own menu item. | The next step |

A cursor file that can't be read is reported the same way, and none of the steps that use it runs after that.

## With an agent

`record_screen` can run the same steps, through its `camera_cutout`, `enhance`, `auto_zoom`, `shortcut_overlays` and `speed_up_typing` arguments, with some differences:

- Every step is off unless the agent asks for it, **Zoom in on clicks** included.
- Every step needs the recording placed on the timeline, so none works with `place: false`.
- `enhance` needs `microphone: true` or a voice-over.
- `shortcut_overlays` and `speed_up_typing` need `keys: "shortcuts"`.
- `auto_zoom`, `shortcut_overlays` and `speed_up_typing` can't be used with a voice-over or a camera on its own.
- Enhance Voice runs at −16 LUFS, not at the Normalize row's target. The agent can call [`enhance_voice`](/mcp/tools-audio/#enhance_voice) afterwards for another target.
- The other steps use the tools' own defaults, never the values a sheet remembers.
- A speed-up that leaves a clip silent above ×12 is applied, and the agent is told.

The call's answer holds each step's own answer, or a sentence under `enhance_error`, `auto_zoom_error` and so on when the step couldn't run. See [Record with record_screen](/mcp/record-screen/#run-steps-after-it-lands).
