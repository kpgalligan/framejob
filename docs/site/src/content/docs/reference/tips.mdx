Frame Job is free to use and its source is open. It's made by one developer, and a tip helps pay for the time to keep working on it. Tipping is voluntary, and nothing in Frame Job is locked without one.

## Leave a tip

Click the green **Tips** button at the right end of the editor's toolbar, after **Export…**. A small window opens, titled Tips, that says who a tip pays for.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Leave a Tip** | Opens the tips page in your browser, then closes the window. <kbd>↩</kbd> presses it. |
| **Not Now** | Closes the window. <kbd>⎋</kbd> presses it, and so does the window's close button. |

Frame Job takes no payment itself. The page in your browser does that, and Frame Job never learns whether you tipped.

The same window opens from **Frame Job ▸ Settings… ▸ Tips**, with or without a project open. It isn't a sheet, so you can keep working with it open.

## When Frame Job asks

Frame Job opens the Tips window by itself rarely, and only just after an export you made has finished.

- **The first time** comes once you've finished at least 5 exports and 30 days have passed since the first of them. If you press **Leave a Tip** before then, that counts as having been asked, and the next time is a December.
- **After that**, once a year, in December. If Frame Job asked, or you pressed **Leave a Tip**, after the start of June, it skips that year's December.

It appears when you close the result of an **Export…**, or when an export you added to the queue finishes. It never takes the keyboard, so a key you were typing can't press one of its buttons. Close it and it won't come back until the next time it's due.

Only exports you start from the export sheet count: **Export…** and **Add to Queue**. An export that fails or that you cancel doesn't count. Exports an agent or the Assistant starts don't count either, and they never open the window.

Opening the window yourself and choosing **Not Now** changes nothing about when Frame Job next asks.

## Turn tips off

Choose **Frame Job ▸ Settings…** and find **Tips**, the last section.

| Control | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Show the Tips button and the yearly reminder** | Shows the green **Tips** button in the toolbar and lets Frame Job open the Tips window by itself. Untick it to stop both. | On |
| **Tips** | Opens the Tips window. It's always there, whatever the box says. | |

The box saves the moment you click it, and the button leaves or returns in every open editor window at once.

## Good to know

- Frame Job keeps the count of your exports, the date of the first one and the date it last asked in macOS's preferences for Frame Job, on your Mac only.
- December is judged by your Mac's time zone.
- If you export nothing in December, Frame Job doesn't ask that year.
- No agent tool opens the Tips window or changes this setting.
