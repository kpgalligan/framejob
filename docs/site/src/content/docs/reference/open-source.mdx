## Frame Job's licence

Frame Job is released under the Apache License, Version 2.0. The full text is the [LICENSE file](https://github.com/kpgalligan/framejob/blob/main/LICENSE) at the root of the repository.

## Third-party notices

Every copy of the app carries `Acknowledgements.txt`, which lists every third-party notice the app owes, each with its licence text. It's written at each build, from the Rust crates, the Assistant helper's Swift package (Slaude), Sparkle, the encoders, the voice-activity model and the fonts.

There's no entry for it in the About panel. To read it:

<Steps>

1. In the Finder, right-click Frame Job and choose **Show Package Contents**.

2. Open `Contents/Resources/Acknowledgements.txt`.

</Steps>

## What carries its own licence

Some of what ships with Frame Job keeps its own licence, beside it in the app or listed in `Acknowledgements.txt`.

| Part | Licence | Where its text is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fonts: Anton, Bebas Neue, Darker Grotesque, IBM Plex Mono, Montserrat, Source Sans 3 and Source Serif 4 | SIL Open Font License | `Contents/Resources/Fonts/`, one `OFL-` file for each family |
| The voice-activity model, Silero VAD | MIT | `Contents/Resources/Models/LICENSE` |
| SVT-AV1 and libvpx, the [AV1 and VP9](/export/av1-and-vp9/) encoders | BSD 3-Clause Clear and BSD 3-Clause | `Contents/Resources/Licenses/svt-av1/` and `Contents/Resources/Licenses/libvpx/`, with their patent texts |
| A patched copy of tiny-skia, which draws [graphics](/graphics/what-is-a-graphic/) | BSD 3-Clause | `Acknowledgements.txt` |

The published app also includes Sparkle, for [updates](/getting-started/updates/). A copy you build yourself has no updater.
