Frame Job is free and open source, and it's made by one developer. If it's useful to you, a contribution helps keep the work going. Contributions are voluntary, and nothing in Frame Job is locked without one.

A contribution here is money you give to support the work. Frame Job takes no payment itself, and it never learns whether you contributed.

## Make a contribution

Click the green **Contribute** button at the right end of the editor's toolbar, after **Export…**. A small window opens, titled Contribute.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Contribute** | Opens the contribution page in your browser, then closes the window. <kbd>↩</kbd> presses it. |
| **Not Now** | Closes the window. <kbd>⎋</kbd> presses it, and so does the window's close button. |

The same window opens from the **Contribute…** button in **Frame Job ▸ Settings… ▸ Contributions**, with or without a project open. It isn't a sheet, so you can keep working with it open.

## When Frame Job asks

Frame Job opens the Contribute window by itself rarely, and only just after an export has finished.

- **The first time** comes once you've finished at least 5 exports and 30 days have passed since the first of them. If you press **Contribute** in the window before then, that counts as having been asked, and the next time is a December.
- **After that**, once a year, in December. If Frame Job asked, or you pressed **Contribute** in the window, after the start of June, it skips that year's December.

It appears when you close the result of an **Export…**, or when an export on the queue finishes: one you added, or one an agent or the Assistant started. It never takes the keyboard, so a key you were typing can't press one of its buttons, and it never holds up an agent's next call. If Frame Job is behind another app at that moment, the window waits there and doesn't bring Frame Job forward. Close it and it won't come back until the next time it's due.

Every export that finishes counts, whoever started it: **Export…** and **Add to Queue** in the export sheet, an agent's [`export`](/mcp/export/) and the Assistant's. An export that fails or is cancelled doesn't count.

Opening the window yourself and choosing **Not Now** changes nothing about when Frame Job next asks.

## Turn the button and the reminder off

Choose **Frame Job ▸ Settings…** and find **Contributions**, the last section.

| Control | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Show the Contribute button and the yearly reminder** | Shows the green **Contribute** button in the toolbar and lets Frame Job open the Contribute window by itself. Untick it to stop both. | On |
| **Contribute…** | Opens the Contribute window. It's always there, whatever the box says. | |

The box saves the moment you click it, and the toolbar button leaves or returns in every open editor window at once.

## Good to know

- Frame Job keeps the count of your exports, the date of the first one and the date it last asked in macOS's preferences for Frame Job, on your Mac only.
- December is judged by your Mac's time zone.
- If you export nothing in December, Frame Job doesn't ask that year.
- No agent tool opens the Contribute window on request or changes this setting. An agent's export finishing can bring the reminder, as yours can.
- A [test copy on its own socket](/mcp/connect-a-client/#testing-a-second-copy) counts no exports and never opens the Contribute window by itself.
