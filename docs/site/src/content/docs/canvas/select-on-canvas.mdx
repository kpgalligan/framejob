## Click a layer

Click the picture to select the front-most visible clip under the pointer at the playhead. The click follows the clip's rotation, so a turned clip is selected by the shape you can see, not by the box it would have upright.

The selected clip's handles come first: a handle lying on top of another clip still grabs the handle, so you can resize a clip that has others in front of it.

Clicking a clip that's already part of a multiple selection keeps the whole selection, so you can drag them together. To narrow it down to one clip, click empty canvas first or use the [timeline](/timeline/selecting-clips/).

## Shift-click

<kbd>⇧</kbd>-click adds a clip to the selection, or takes it out if it's already selected.

## Clear the selection

Click empty canvas, or the area around it, to clear the selection. This also leaves [crop mode](/canvas/crop/).

With <kbd>⇧</kbd> held the selection stays, and crop mode still ends.

## The selection frame

Every selected clip that's drawing at the playhead gets a coral outline with a dark backing line, so it shows on both light and dark pictures. The front-most selected clip has a heavier outline and carries the handles:

- Eight white square handles, at the corners and the middle of each edge, for [scaling](/canvas/move-scale-rotate/#scale).
- A round grip on a stalk above the top edge, for [rotating](/canvas/move-scale-rotate/#rotate).
- On a [line or arrow](/text-and-shapes/lines-and-arrows/), a round handle at each end as well.

The handles stay the same size on screen at every zoom. The pointer shows what a handle does: a left-right or up-down resize pointer on an edge handle, and a crosshair on a corner.

A selected clip that isn't at the playhead has no frame, because it isn't in the picture. An adjustment clip never has one, because it has no rectangle of its own. A nest clip's frame is the rectangle it's placed in, and a click selects it only where something inside it draws. See [Layers](/canvas/the-canvas/#layers).

## Matte clips

A clip on a [matte track](/canvas/matte-tracks/) can be selected and dragged inside its own box, even though it draws nothing there.

Because it's in front of the clip it cuts, a click inside that box selects the matte clip. To select the clip it cuts, click outside the matte clip's box, or use the timeline.

## Good to know

Clips on hidden tracks can't be clicked, and clips on locked tracks are passed over. See [Hidden and locked tracks](/canvas/the-canvas/#hidden-and-locked-tracks).
