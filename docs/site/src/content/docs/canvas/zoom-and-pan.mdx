Zoom the preview to look closely at the picture or to place something precisely, then pan to reach the part you want. Zooming is only a way of looking: it changes nothing in the project, makes no undo step and is never exported. To zoom in on part of the video so your viewers see it, use [Zoom to Region](/motion/zoom-to-region/) or [Auto Zoom](/recording/auto-zoom/).

## The zoom popup

The popup in the toolbar sets how large the picture appears. Its stops are **Fit**, **50%**, **100%** and **200%**. It always shows the zoom the canvas is at, however you got there.

**Fit** sizes the canvas to the pane and follows the pane as it changes. At **100%**, one canvas pixel takes one point on screen.

Each window starts at **Fit**, and the zoom isn't saved with the project.

## View menu commands

| Command | Key | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **View ▸ Zoom In** | <kbd>⌘+</kbd> or <kbd>⌘=</kbd> | Goes to the next stop up. Greyed at 200%. |
| **View ▸ Zoom Out** | <kbd>⌘-</kbd> | Goes to the next stop down. Greyed at Fit. |
| **View ▸ Zoom to Fit** | <kbd>⌘0</kbd> | Goes straight back to Fit and centres the canvas. Greyed at Fit. |

These zoom the preview. To zoom the timeline, see [Zoom and scroll the timeline](/timeline/zoom-and-scroll/).

## Pinch and scroll

Pinch on a trackpad over the preview, or scroll with <kbd>⌥</kbd> or <kbd>⌘</kbd> held, to step through the same four stops. The stops are separate steps, so a gesture has to build up a little before it takes the next one.

## Panning

When the zoomed canvas is bigger than the pane, there are two ways to move it:

- Scroll over the preview.
- Hold <kbd>Space</kbd> and drag. If you've been working elsewhere, click the canvas first, or <kbd>Space</kbd> plays and pauses instead of panning.

You can only pan in a direction where the canvas overflows the pane, so you can't drag the canvas out of view. While the canvas fits, a plain scroll does nothing.

Moving between stops keeps your pan, pulled back if it no longer fits. Going back to **Fit** centres the canvas again.

## Space on the canvas

After you've clicked the canvas, <kbd>Space</kbd> still plays and pauses. Frame Job can't tell a tap from a pan until you let go, so playback starts or stops on the release rather than the press. If you panned while <kbd>Space</kbd> was down, the release does nothing to playback.

## Good to know

- The stops count up from **Fit** in order, whatever size Fit happens to be. On a small canvas in a large pane, **50%** can be smaller than **Fit**.
- While the canvas fits, holding <kbd>Space</kbd> and dragging a clip moves the clip as usual, and playback toggles when you let go of <kbd>Space</kbd>.
