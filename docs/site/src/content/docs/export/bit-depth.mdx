The **Bit depth** row sets how finely a WAV or AIFF file records each sample of the sound. It's about sound only. The depth of the picture comes from the format you choose, and is on [Precision and bit depth](/hdr/precision/).

## Choose a bit depth

In the export sheet, choose **Audio only (WAV)** or **Audio only (AIFF)** in **Format**, then pick a row in **Bit depth**, which sits under **Quality**.

| Row | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **16-bit** | The default. About 11.5 MB a minute. |
| **24-bit** | Keeps detail 16-bit can't. It matters most for a quiet recording you're going to raise or process later. About 17.3 MB a minute. |

A 24-bit file shows `24-bit` in the footnote, for example `LPCM · WAV · 24-bit`.

Both depths are rounded once from the [float mix](/export/audio-only/#the-48-khz-stereo-master), to the nearest value, and neither is dithered.

## Good to know

- On every other format **Bit depth** is greyed out and keeps your last choice.
- ProRes sound stays 16-bit, and AAC and Opus sound is encoded from 16-bit samples.
- The choice is remembered with the rest of the sheet and stored in [presets](/export/presets/).

## With an agent

An agent passes `audio_bits` to `export`, as 16 or 24. It works on `audio_wav` and `audio_aiff` only, and any other format returns an error. See [Export: start, then poll](/mcp/export/#audio-bits).
