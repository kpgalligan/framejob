## Add a shape clip

<Steps>

1. Move the playhead to where the shape should start.

2. Click **Add Shape** in the timeline bar.

3. Choose **Rectangle**, **Ellipse**, **Line** or **Arrow**.

</Steps>

**Add Shape** has no menu item and no key. **Clip ▸ Add Background ▸ Solid Color** also makes a shape clip, a white rectangle the canvas's size on a new track at the back. See [Backgrounds](/canvas/backgrounds/).

The shape goes on the front-most track if there's room at the playhead, and otherwise on a new track in front of everything. With a [nest](/editing/nests/) open, it goes in the nest. Adding it is one undo step, named after the kind, such as "Add Rectangle".

A new shape:

- lasts 5 seconds, and has no source file, so you can [trim](/editing/trimming/) it to any length
- is a 320 × 120 px box with a 6 px white stroke and no fill, so it shows up over any footage
- is selected, so the **Shape** sub-tab opens on it

Everything about how it's drawn is on [Shape fill, stroke and size](/text-and-shapes/shape-settings/).

## The four kinds

You can change a shape's kind at any time with [**Kind**](/text-and-shapes/shape-settings/#change-the-kind).

### Rectangle

A rectangle that fills its box. A rounded rectangle is a Rectangle with a [corner radius](/text-and-shapes/shape-settings/#round-a-rectangles-corners), not a separate kind.

### Ellipse

An ellipse that fills its box. Make the box square for a circle.

### Line

A straight line. A new line runs diagonally across its box, from bottom left to top right. Drag the round handle at either end to move that end. See [Lines and arrows](/text-and-shapes/lines-and-arrows/).

### Arrow

A line with an arrowhead. A new arrow runs diagonally across its box like a line, with its head at the end turned on.

## Resize with the handles

Dragging a shape's handle changes the size of its box, not a scale factor, so the stroke stays the same thickness. The box's new size shows in the **Width** and **Height** fields.

| Gesture | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Drag a corner handle | Resizes the box and keeps its aspect ratio. |
| <kbd>⇧</kbd>-drag a corner handle | Frees the aspect ratio, so width and height move independently. |
| Drag a side handle | Changes only the width or only the height. |
| <kbd>⌥</kbd>-drag a handle | Resizes about the centre rather than pinning the opposite handle. |

The drag holds each side of the box between 4 and 20,000 px.

<kbd>⇧</kbd> is special to shapes here. A video or picture has one uniform scale, so <kbd>⇧</kbd> changes nothing on those. For the rest of the canvas gestures, see [Move, scale and rotate](/canvas/move-scale-rotate/).

A box resize always changes the clip itself, even with an [action](/motion/actions/) selected. To animate a shape's size, use **Scale** in the [Transform](/canvas/transform/) section, which enlarges the whole drawing, stroke included.

## Picture sections

A shape clip has three sub-tabs: **Shape**, **Picture** and **Clip**. Its **Picture** sub-tab has these sections, in this order:

- [Transform](/canvas/transform/)
- [Behind Subject](/effects/text-behind-subject/), on the top-level timeline only, so not while a nest is open
- [Hide While Silent](/effects/hide-while-silent/)
- [Shadow](/effects/shadow/)
- [Filters](/effects/filters/)
- [Reflection](/effects/reflection/)
- [Spotlight](/effects/spotlight/)
- [Obscure](/effects/obscure/)

Crop, Corner Radius, Chroma Key and Background aren't offered, and neither is the **Color** sub-tab. To round a shape's corners, use **Corner radius** on the **Shape** sub-tab.

## With an agent

`add_shape_clip` puts a shape on the track and at the time you name, and can give it its length and look in the same step. It can also make a shape larger than the canvas, which starts scaled to fit when [**Fit new clips to canvas**](/projects/background-and-timecode/#fit-new-clips-to-canvas) is on. An arrow starts with its head on, so pass `arrow_end: false` for a bare diagonal. See [Tools: adding clips](/mcp/tools-adding-clips/#add_shape_clip).
