The **Shape** sub-tab sets what a [shape clip](/text-and-shapes/shape-clips/) is and how it's painted. Where it sits, how large it's scaled and how see-through it is as a whole are on the **Picture** sub-tab, in [Transform](/canvas/transform/).

## Change the kind

**Kind** switches the shape between **Rectangle**, **Ellipse**, **Line** and **Arrow**. It changes only the kind, so everything else stays as it was.

Rows that don't apply to the new kind are greyed, not lost, so flipping the kind back finds them as you left them.

| Row | Available for |
| --- | --- |
| **Corner radius** | Rectangle |
| The **Gradient** section | Rectangle, Ellipse |
| The **Arrowheads** section | Line, Arrow |

A shape switched to **Arrow** keeps its own arrowhead setting, so a rectangle switched to Arrow has no head until you tick one. Only a shape added as an Arrow starts with a head.

## Set the size

**Width** and **Height** set the shape's box in pixels, from 1 to 20,000 px each. A new shape is 320 × 120 px. A number typed outside the range is brought back to the nearest limit. However large the box is, a shape is drawn at no more than 8,192 px on a side.

[Dragging a handle](/text-and-shapes/shape-clips/#resize-with-the-handles) on the canvas changes the same two numbers. **Scale** in [Transform](/canvas/transform/) is different: it enlarges the finished drawing, stroke included.

## Round a rectangle's corners

**Corner radius** rounds a rectangle's corners, from 0 to 2,000 px, with quick picks at 0, 4, 8, 16, 32 and 64. The default is 0. It's greyed for other kinds.

A radius can't go past half the box's shorter side. At that point the short ends are fully round, and a larger number looks the same.

The **Shape** section's **Reset** puts the corner radius back to 0, and leaves the kind and the box alone.

## Draw an outline

The **Stroke** section draws an outline around the shape. A new shape has one.

| Control | What it does | Default | Range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Stroke** | Turns the outline on or off. | On | |
| **Width** | How thick it is. Quick picks 1, 2, 4, 6, 12 and 24. | 6 px | 0.5 to 200 px |
| **Color** | Its colour. | White | |

**Width** and **Color** are greyed while the stroke is off. Turning the stroke off forgets its width and colour, so turning it on again gives 6 px white. **Reset** also goes back to 6 px white rather than turning the stroke off.

On a rectangle or ellipse the stroke sits inside the box, so a thicker stroke doesn't make the shape bigger.

When the section is closed, its header reads the width, such as "6.0 px", or "off".

## Fill the shape

The **Fill** section paints the inside of a rectangle or ellipse with a flat colour. A new shape has no fill.

| Control | What it does | Default | Range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Fill** | Turns the fill on or off. | Off | |
| **Color** | The fill's colour. | White, once turned on | |
| **Opacity** | How solid the fill is. Quick picks 0, 25, 50, 75 and 100. | 100 % | 0 to 100 % |

**Color** and **Opacity** are greyed while the fill is off. Turning the fill off forgets its colour, so turning it on again gives opaque white. **Reset** turns the fill off.

**Opacity** belongs to the fill alone, so it doesn't fade the stroke. To fade the whole shape, use **Opacity** in [Transform](/canvas/transform/).

When the section is closed, its header reads the fill's opacity, such as "100%", or "off".

For a colour ramp in place of a flat colour, see [Gradient fills](/text-and-shapes/gradients/). A gradient covers the fill while it's on, and turning it off brings the fill back.

## Add arrowheads

The **Arrowheads** section has two separate boxes, **Arrowhead at start** and **Arrowhead at end**. They're available for lines and arrows, and greyed for rectangles and ellipses.

:::note
Arrowheads are drawn only when **Kind** is **Arrow**. On a Line you can tick the boxes and they're kept, but no head appears until you switch the kind to Arrow.
:::

When the section is closed, its header reads "both", "start", "end" or "none". See [Lines and arrows](/text-and-shapes/lines-and-arrows/) for moving the ends.

A line or arrow is drawn with its stroke, so a Line with **Stroke** off shows nothing. **Fill** paints nothing on a line or arrow, although its rows stay available. The one exception is an Arrow with **Stroke** off, whose heads take the fill colour.

## Why shape colours don't animate

An [action](/motion/actions/) can't change a shape's kind, size, corner radius, stroke, fill, gradient or arrowheads. These rows always change the clip itself, so while an action is selected each one shows ⊘, and each section says "Actions don't animate this. Edits here change the clip."

A shape's position, scale and opacity animate like any clip's. To move or fade a shape, select an action and use the [Transform](/canvas/transform/) rows on the **Picture** sub-tab. See [What an action can animate](/motion/what-can-animate/).

## With an agent

`set_shape` changes any of these settings in one call and leaves the rest as they are, including a line's two ends. Setting `stroke` or `fill` to `null` removes it. A shape's position and scale aren't part of it: those go through `set_video`. To change a shape inside a nest, see [Editing inside a nest](/mcp/nests/). For every key, see [Tools: text, shapes and clip names](/mcp/tools-clip-properties/#set_shape).
