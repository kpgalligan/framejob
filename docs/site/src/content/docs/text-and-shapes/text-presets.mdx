A preset gives a text clip a finished look with one click: its font, size, alignment, backdrop and position, sized to fit your canvas.

## Apply a preset

<Steps>

1. Select one or more text clips.

2. Open the **Presets** section at the bottom of the **Text** sub-tab. It starts closed.

3. Click **Title**, **Subtitle**, **Lower Third** or **Caption**.

</Steps>

It's one undo step, named after the preset.

## The four presets

| Preset | Font | Size | At 1920 × 1080 | Align | Position | Backdrop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Title** | Source Sans 3 Bold | 9 % | 97 px | Centre | Centre of the canvas | None |
| **Subtitle** | Source Sans 3 SemiBold | 5 % | 54 px | Centre | 18 % of the height below centre | None |
| **Lower Third** | Source Sans 3 SemiBold | 4.5 % | 49 px | Left | 20 % of the width left of centre, 28 % of the height below it | Black at 55 %, padding 18 px, radius 6 px |
| **Caption** | Source Sans 3 Regular | 3.5 % | 38 px | Centre | 38 % of the height below centre | Black at 60 %, padding 12 px, radius 8 px |

The position is where the centre of the text's box sits, so a longer name on a **Lower Third** grows both ways from that point.

**Title** isn't the look a new text clip starts with. That has the same font at a fixed 72 px, where **Title** works its size out from the canvas.

On a canvas taller than wide, **Caption** sits higher, a quarter of the height below centre, clear of a phone's on-screen controls.

[Subtitles](/captions/subtitles/) from **File ▸ Generate Captions…** and **File ▸ Import Captions…** use the **Caption** look, and they also wrap at 80 % of the canvas width. The **Caption** button itself leaves the [box](/text-and-shapes/text-box/) as it is.

## How a preset fits the canvas

Sizes are a share of the canvas's shorter side, and positions a share of its width and height. So a preset fits any canvas, and a vertical 1080 × 1920 canvas gets the same text height as a 1920 × 1080 one. A backdrop's padding and radius stay at the pixel values in the table.

The size is worked out once, from the canvas at the moment you click. Changing the canvas afterwards doesn't apply the preset again.

## What a preset changes

As well as the settings in the table, a preset sets **Vertical** to middle and **Opacity** back to 100 %. **Title** and **Subtitle** take away any backdrop.

It leaves everything else alone, including the words, the colour, the outline, tracking, line spacing, **UPPERCASE**, the box, the reveal, scale and rotation.

With an [action](/motion/actions/) selected, a preset sets that action's end, like any other field.

## With an agent

There's no preset tool. An agent sends the same values through `set_text` and `set_video`. See [Tools: text, shapes and clip names](/mcp/tools-clip-properties/#set_text).
