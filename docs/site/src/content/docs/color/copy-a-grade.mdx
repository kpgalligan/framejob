When one clip looks right, Paste Attributes gives its grade to any number of other clips in one undo step. This page covers what that means for a grade. [Paste Attributes](/editing/paste-attributes/) covers the command in full.

## Copy a grade with Paste Attributes

<Steps>

1. Select the graded clip and press <kbd>⌘C</kbd>.

2. Select the clips that should take the grade.

3. Press <kbd>⌥⌘V</kbd>, or choose **Edit ▸ Paste Attributes**.

</Steps>

The copied clip's video settings replace each selected clip's, as one undo step called Paste Attributes. If nothing would change, Frame Job beeps.

## What comes with the grade

There's no way to paste the grade alone, because it travels with the rest of the clip's picture settings:

- Light, Color, Wheels, Curves, and the look with its **Amount**
- filters
- everything else about the picture, including position and scale
- the audio settings too, where the copied clip has sound and the target can take them

A clip's place, speed, actions, transitions, name and label never come across.

:::caution
Position and scale come with the grade. Pasting onto a clip you've already placed moves it to where the copied clip sits. To share a grade without touching placement, put an [adjustment clip](/color/adjustment-clips/) above the clips instead.
:::

The paste replaces, it doesn't merge, so anything the target had that the copied clip didn't is gone. A grade that lives in an [action](/color/animate-a-grade/) doesn't travel either, because actions stay behind. The target takes the copied clip's own values, not its animation.

## LUTs travel with a paste

A look that uses an imported [`.cube` LUT](/color/luts/) keeps working when you paste it into another project, with Paste Attributes or with **Edit ▸ Paste**. The LUT arrives in that project's media along with the clip, and if the project already has the same file, the paste uses that one.

A pasted look never lands on the wrong file. If what's being pasted doesn't carry the LUT, the clip gets no look.

## Keep a grade for later

A clip holds one grade. There are no grade versions, no gallery of stills and no way to save a grade or a look of your own, and Frame Job can't export a grade as a `.cube` LUT.

A grade can still move between projects, because [Paste Attributes](/editing/paste-attributes/) works across them. Copy the graded clip in one project and paste onto clips in another. A project of graded clips you keep for this works as a library.

## A qualifier between clips

A [qualifier](/color/qualifiers/) belongs to an adjustment clip, so what happens to it depends on the clips at both ends of the paste.

| Pasting | The qualifier |
| --- | --- |
| Onto a picture clip | Is dropped |
| From a picture clip onto an adjustment clip | Stays as the adjustment clip had it |
| From one adjustment clip onto another | Is copied |

## When a paste stops

An adjustment clip has no picture of its own, so some settings can't land on it. If the copied clip has a blur, sharpen or pixelate filter, or a blend mode, pasting onto an adjustment clip shows an error naming the clip and the setting, and nothing changes on any selected clip.

Sepia, noir, vignette and invert filters paste onto an adjustment clip without trouble.

To get past it, take the setting off the copied clip and copy it again, or leave the adjustment clip out of the selection. [When a paste stops](/editing/paste-attributes/#when-a-paste-stops) has the full list, for every kind of clip.

## With an agent

The menu item always copies each of the picture, sound, text and shape groups both clips have. An agent can list the ones it wants in `paste_attributes`'s `groups`, out of `video`, `audio`, `text`, `shape` and `graphic`, so `["video"]` brings the picture settings and leaves the sound alone. The grade is still part of `video`, so to set a grade and nothing else, an agent uses `set_video`. See [Tools: copying and nesting](/mcp/tools-copy-and-nest/#paste_attributes).
