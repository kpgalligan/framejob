Frame Job runs on Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 26 or later. There's no account to make. Every AI feature runs on your own API key, and everything else works without one.

## What you can do

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Cut" icon="seti:video">
    [Trim](/editing/trimming/), [slip and roll](/editing/slip-and-roll/),
    [split](/editing/splitting/) and [nest](/editing/nests/) clips on tracks that hold anything.
    Take out dead air with [Remove Silences](/cleanup/remove-silences/) and
    [Remove Filler Words](/cleanup/remove-filler-words/), or
    [cut by deleting words](/captions/delete-words/) from the transcript.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Compose" icon="seti:image">
    Place [text](/text-and-shapes/text-clips/), [shapes](/text-and-shapes/shape-clips/) and
    [pictures](/text-and-shapes/image-clips/) on a fixed canvas, use
    [matte tracks](/canvas/matte-tracks/), and
    [Remove Background](/effects/remove-background/) with no green screen.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Animate" icon="rocket">
    Ease any property with [actions](/motion/actions/), use
    [motion presets](/motion/motion-presets/) and [transitions](/motion/transitions/), build
    [speed ramps](/speed/speed-ramps/), and drop in [motion graphics](/graphics/what-is-a-graphic/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Grade" icon="seti:favicon">
    [Wheels](/color/wheels/), [curves](/color/curves/), [looks](/color/looks/),
    [LUTs](/color/luts/), [qualifiers](/color/qualifiers/) and
    [scopes](/color/scopes/), with [camera log](/hdr/camera-log/) and
    [HDR projects](/hdr/dynamic-range/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mix" icon="seti:audio">
    [Enhance Voice](/audio/enhance-voice/), [EQ](/audio/eq/),
    [ducking](/audio/ducking/), [Normalize](/audio/normalize/) to a loudness target, and
    [Synchronize Audio](/audio/synchronize-audio/) across cameras and recorders.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Caption" icon="comment-alt">
    [Generate captions](/captions/generate-captions/) on your Mac or with AssemblyAI, as
    [subtitles](/captions/subtitles/) or [word-by-word styles](/captions/word-caption-styles/),
    and read your project as [a transcript](/captions/transcript-tab/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Record" icon="laptop">
    Record [a display](/recording/record-a-display/) or
    [a window](/recording/record-a-window/) with [a camera beside it](/recording/camera-beside-the-screen/),
    then [Auto Zoom](/recording/auto-zoom/) and
    [Speed Up Typing](/recording/speed-up-typing/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reframe" icon="seti:crystal">
    [Follow Subject](/framing/follow-subject/) keeps a person in frame, and
    [Make Vertical Version](/framing/make-vertical-version/) turns a whole edit into 9:16, 4:5 or
    1:1. [Switch by Speaker](/multicam/switch-by-speaker/) cuts between cameras for you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Deliver" icon="seti:config">
    [H.264 and HEVC](/export/h264-and-hevc/), [ProRes](/export/prores/),
    [AV1 and VP9](/export/av1-and-vp9/), [transparent exports](/export/transparency/),
    [stems](/export/stems/) and an [Export Queue](/export/export-queue/) that runs behind the editor.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
