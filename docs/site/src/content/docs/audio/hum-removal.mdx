Hum removal cuts the low electrical hum that mains power can leave on a recording. It places a narrow notch at the mains frequency and at each of its multiples, and leaves the sound between the notches alone.

<AtAGlance where="Clip tab ▸ Sound ▸ Hum" tools="set_audio" />

## Remove hum

<Steps>

1. Select a clip with sound, and open the **Hum** section of the [Sound tab](/audio/sound-tab/).

2. Tick **Remove hum**. It starts at 60 Hz, 30 dB deep, with 4 harmonics.

3. Set **Mains** to the frequency where the recording was made: 50 Hz in most of the world, 60 Hz in most of the Americas and parts of Asia.

4. If you can still hear the hum, raise **Depth** or **Harmonics**.

</Steps>

The other controls are greyed out until **Remove hum** is ticked. **Reset** in the section's header turns hum removal off.

## The controls

| Control | What it does | Range | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Remove hum** | Turns hum removal on. | On or off | Off |
| **Mains** | The mains frequency where the recording was made. | 50 Hz or 60 Hz | 60 Hz |
| **Depth** | How far each notch turns the hum down. Quick picks: 12, 24, 30 and 48 dB. | 6 to 60 dB | 30 dB |
| **Harmonics** | How many notches: the mains frequency and its multiples. Quick picks: 1, 2, 4 and 8. | 1 to 8 | 4 |

At 60 Hz with 4 harmonics, the notches sit at 60, 120, 180 and 240 Hz.

Each notch is about a tenth of the mains frequency wide, whatever its depth, so a notch at 60 Hz is about 6 Hz wide.

## Good to know

- Hum removal runs after the [EQ](/audio/eq/). Its notches appear in the EQ section's drawn curve, but the EQ's **Bypass** doesn't turn them off.
- Hum removal only reaches the mains frequency and its multiples. For hiss, fans or other steady noise, use [Noise Reduction](/audio/noise-reduction/), which comes after it.
- With an [action](/audio/animate-sound/) selected, these controls set where the action ends. **Depth** glides across the action. **Mains**, **Harmonics** and turning hum removal on or off change at the action's end, with no glide.
- Hum removal set on a [nest](/editing/nests/) clip has no effect. Open the nest with <kbd>⌘↓</kbd> and set it on the clips inside.

## With an agent

`set_audio` sets the same controls through `hum`, with `frequency`, `depth_db` and `harmonics`. An empty `hum` turns it on at the defaults and `null` turns it off. See [Tools: clip sound](/mcp/tools-set-audio/#hum-removal).
