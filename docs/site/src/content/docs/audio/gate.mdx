The gate turns a clip down in its pauses. When the sound falls below the threshold it's pushed further down, so room noise and breaths between phrases get quieter while the speech itself is untouched. Above the threshold the gate is open and leaves the sound alone. It turns the pauses down, it doesn't cut them out. To take pauses out of the timeline, use [Remove Silences](/cleanup/remove-silences/).

## Gate the pauses

<Steps>

1. Select a clip with sound, and open the **Gate** section of the [Sound tab](/audio/sound-tab/).

2. Tick **Gate pauses**. It starts at the [defaults](#the-controls), which work from the clip's own loudness.

3. Play the clip. If the ends of words are being cut, raise **Hold** or lower **Threshold**.

4. If the pauses aren't quiet enough, raise **Range**. If they sound too empty, lower it.

</Steps>

The other controls are greyed out until **Gate pauses** is ticked. **Reset** in the section's header turns the gate off.

## The controls

| Control | What it does | Range | Default | Quick picks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Threshold** | How far under the clip's loudness the gate opens. | −40 to 0 dB | −10 dB | −20, −15, −10, −6 |
| **Ratio** | How strongly sound under the threshold is turned down. At 4:1, each dB under the threshold becomes 3 dB further down. | 1:1 to 20:1 | 4:1 | 2, 4, 10, 20 |
| **Range** | The most the gate ever takes away. | 1 to 80 dB | 20 dB | 10, 20, 40, 80 |
| **Attack** | How quickly it opens when the sound rises. | 0 to 100 ms | 1 ms | 1, 5, 10 |
| **Hold** | How long it stays open after the sound falls, so the ends of words aren't clipped. | 0 to 500 ms | 50 ms | 0, 50, 100, 200 |
| **Release** | How quickly it closes after the hold. | 1 to 2,000 ms | 100 ms | 50, 100, 250, 500 |

## The threshold follows the clip

**Threshold** is set in dB under the clip's own loudness, not at a fixed level, so the same setting works on a quiet recording and a loud one. A trim, a pan change or a different channel choice moves the loudness, and the threshold moves with it.

That loudness is the Loudness figure in the [Volume](/audio/volume/) section. It's measured before noise reduction, the compressor, the volume, the fades and ducking, so moving the Volume slider doesn't change where the gate opens.

## Read Status and Loudness

Two readouts at the foot of the section show what the threshold means for this clip. With several clips selected, **Loudness** reads "—". **Status** does too, unless the gate is off on the first of them, when it reads "off".

**Loudness** is the level the threshold is measured from, for example "−21.8 LUFS". It reads "measuring…" until the clip has been measured.

**Status** says where the gate opens, or why it isn't working:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| off | **Gate pauses** isn't ticked. |
| Opens above −31.8 dBFS | The clip's loudness plus the **Threshold**. Here, −21.8 LUFS with a −10 dB threshold. |
| Waits for its loudness | The clip hasn't been measured yet. See below. |
| Not gating: too short | The clip plays for under 0.4 s, too short to measure. |
| Not gating: too quiet | The clip is too quiet to measure. |
| Not gating: no sound | The clip plays no sound in this timeline, or it's a nest clip. |

## Before the clip's loudness is measured

The gate, the [leveller](/audio/leveller/) and the [de-esser](/audio/de-esser/) all depend on the clip's measured loudness. Until it's measured, the clip plays without them, and the preview picks them up by itself.

An export waits until every gated, levelled or de-essed clip has been measured. After five minutes without a measurement it stops, saying that the clip's loudness or speech hasn't been measured yet, and to wait for the Status rows to show the clip's loudness and export again.

A clip that can't be measured, because it's under 0.4 s or too quiet, plays and exports without them, and the export doesn't wait for it.

## Good to know

- The gate runs after [noise reduction](/audio/noise-reduction/) and before the leveller, the de-esser and the [compressor](/audio/compressor/). See [How audio works](/audio/how-audio-works/).
- The gate doesn't look ahead, so a loud start after a long pause can lose its first moment while the gate opens. At the default **Attack**, that's about a millisecond.
- There's no way to listen to only what the gate takes away, so judge it by playing the clip.
- With an [action](/audio/animate-sound/) selected, these controls set where the action ends. The numbers glide across the action, and turning the gate on or off changes at the action's end.
- The gate does nothing on a [nest](/editing/nests/) clip. Open the nest with <kbd>⌘↓</kbd> and set it on the clips inside.

## With an agent

`set_audio` sets the same controls through `gate`, with `threshold_db`, `ratio`, `range_db`, `attack`, `hold` and `release`. The three times use an [agent's time units](/mcp/units-and-ids/), not milliseconds. An empty `gate` turns it on at the defaults and `null` turns it off. See [Tools: clip sound](/mcp/tools-set-audio/#gate).
