## Insert a template

<Steps>

1. Move the playhead to where the graphic should start.

2. Choose **Insert ▸ Graphic…**, then a category, then a template. The preview plays it on a loop at your project's canvas shape.

3. Choose **Insert**, press <kbd>Return</kbd>, or double-click the template.

</Steps>

The clip lands at the playhead, on the front track when it fits there, and otherwise on a new track above everything else. [Insert a graphic](/graphics/insert-a-graphic/) covers the whole sheet.

## Change what a template shows

Select the clip and open the **Graphic** sub-tab of the **Clip** tab. **Properties** has one row for each property the template offers, under the template's own short name, such as **boxWidth**. A number with a range is a slider and a field, a number without one is a field, a yes-or-no choice is a checkbox and a fixed set of choices is a pop-up.

See [Graphic properties](/graphics/properties/) for how the rows behave, and [Animate graphic properties](/graphics/animate-properties/) to change a value over time.

## How long a template runs

The default length is in frames at your project's frame rate, so a 150-frame template is 5 seconds in a 30 fps project and 2.5 seconds in a 60 fps one.

To change how long a graphic runs, drag the clip's end on the timeline. [**Follow Clip**](/graphics/size-and-timing/#follow-clip) is on for a new clip, so the exit moves with the clip's end and the entrance still plays from the first frame. A few templates also time something else by the clip's length:

| Template | What follows the clip's length |
| --- | --- |
| Counter | Reaches its final number about 70 % of the way through. |
| Progress bar | Reaches its value about 60 % of the way through. |
| Subscribe button | The first click lands 40 % of the way through. |

[Countdown](#countdown) and [News ticker](#news-ticker) run in real time whatever the clip's length. To fix a graphic's length so it no longer follows the clip, see [The graphic's own timeline](/graphics/size-and-timing/#the-graphics-own-timeline).

## Titles & Text

### Chapter card

A card with a large outlined chapter number, a kicker beside it, an accent rule drawing across and the title rising into view.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **number** | The chapter number, 0 to 99, always shown as two digits. | 1 |
| **kicker** | The small text beside the number. It wraps, shrinking to stay within three lines, down to a smallest size. | CHAPTER |
| **title** | The title under the rule, fitted to the width. | Getting started |
| **background** | The card's colour. | `#0b0b10` |
| **accent** | The number's outline and the rule. | `#ff5a36` |

### Kinetic type

A sentence shown word by word, each word springing in, on a near-black card. Every fifth word, counting from the fourth, takes the accent colour.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **text** | The sentence. | Make something people want to watch twice, then cut it in half |
| **stagger** | Frames between one word and the next, 1 to 30. | 4 |
| **color** | Most of the words. | `#ffffff` |
| **accent** | The fourth word, the ninth, the fourteenth and so on. | `#ffd60a` |

The sentence wraps at 80 % of the canvas's width, at one fixed size, and centres on the frame. A long one can run off the top and bottom, so split it across two clips. The card's colour isn't a property.

### Logo reveal

A card on which an accent bar sweeps across, revealing your logo or a text mark. The bar then settles into a short rule, and a tagline fades up under it.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **logo** | A picture to reveal. **None** draws the **mark** instead. | None |
| **mark** | The text to reveal when there's no picture, fitted to the width. | FRAME JOB |
| **tagline** | The line under the rule. Leave it empty for none. | Edit at the speed of thought |
| **background** | The card's colour. | `#0b0b10` |
| **accent** | The bar and the rule. | `#ff5a36` |

The **logo** pop-up lists the pictures added to the project's graphics, so add yours first with **File ▸ Add Image to Graphics…**. See [Fonts and pictures in graphics](/graphics/fonts-and-pictures/). The picture keeps its proportions inside a box half the width of the title-safe area.

### Lower third

A name and a subtitle that slide in from the left and fade out, on a dark bar with an accent edge.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Title** | The name. | Jane Doe |
| **subtitle** | The line under it. | Director of Photography |
| **accent** | The bar at the left edge. | `#ff5a36` |
| **size** | The size of the name, 12 to 200. The subtitle is half of it. | 64 |
| **boxed** | Draws the dark box behind the text. The accent bar stays either way. | On |
| **align** | Puts the whole block `left`, `center` or `right`. The text inside it stays left-aligned. | `left` |

The box grows to fit the longer line, and the text isn't shrunk to fit. A long name at a large **size** can run off the frame.

### News ticker

A band along the foot of the frame with a headline box at its left and items crawling right to left, with an accent dot between them. Type the items in one field, separated by a vertical bar (`|`). They repeat for as long as the clip runs.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **headline** | The text in the box. Leave it empty for no box. | BREAKING |
| **items** | The items, separated by a vertical bar. | Three sample lines |
| **speed** | How fast they crawl, 40 to 600, in pixels a second when the canvas's shorter side is 1080, and in proportion on others. | 160 |
| **background** | The band's colour, slightly see-through. | `#101014` |
| **accent** | The headline box, the band's top edge and the dots. | `#e5322d` |

### Quote card

A card with a large quote mark, the quote's lines fading up in turn, and a short rule and the attribution sliding in.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **quote** | The quote, in a text area. | Good editing is invisible. All anyone should notice is the story. |
| **attribution** | Who said it, drawn after a dash. Leave it empty for no name. | The Editor |
| **background** | The card's colour. | `#101014` |
| **accent** | The quote mark and the rule. | `#ffd60a` |

The quote shrinks and wraps to fit the frame. It wraps by itself, so line breaks you type are treated as spaces.

### Title card

A title fitted to the frame with a kicker above it, over a diagonal two-colour gradient. The text fades in and rises, then fades out over the last 18 frames, and the gradient stays.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **title** | The title, fitted to 80 % of the canvas's width. | The Long Road to a Motion Graphic |
| **kicker** | The small line above it. | CHAPTER ONE |
| **from** | The gradient's top-left colour. | `#1b2a6b` |
| **to** | The gradient's bottom-right colour. | `#c2185b` |

## Screencast

### Arrow highlight

Points at something on a screen recording: a rounded box drawn on around a spot, an arrow growing toward it from the side nearer the frame's centre, and a label at the arrow's tail.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **x**, **y** | The spot, as a fraction of the frame's width and height, 0 to 1. | 0.62, 0.42 |
| **boxWidth**, **boxHeight** | The box's size, as a fraction of the frame, 0.02 to 0.8. | 0.22, 0.14 |
| **shape** | `box+arrow`, `box` or `arrow`. | `box+arrow` |
| **label** | The text. Leave it empty for none. | Look here |
| **accent** | The box and the arrow. | `#ffd60a` |

With `box` the label sits under the box, or above it when there's no room. With `arrow` the arrow points at the spot itself and the two box sizes do nothing. The box, arrow and label are always kept inside the title-safe area, so a spot near the edge is moved in.

### Callout

A circle that draws itself round a spot, with an arrow and a label.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **x**, **y** | The circle's centre, as a fraction of the frame's width and height, 0 to 1. | 0.62, 0.4 |
| **label** | The text at the arrow's tail. | Click here first |
| **color** | The circle and the arrow. | `#ff3b30` |

The arrow always comes from the lower left, and nothing keeps it on the frame. Near the left or bottom edge the arrow and label run off, so use [Arrow highlight](#arrow-highlight) there.

### Keyboard keys

A shortcut drawn as keycaps that pop in one after another and are then pressed in turn, with a label under them.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **keys** | The keys, joined by `+`. | cmd+shift+4 |
| **label** | The line under the keys. Leave it empty for none. | Screenshot a selection |
| **theme** | `light` or `dark` keycaps. | `light` |
| **position** | `bottom`, `center` or `top`. | `bottom` |
| **accent** | The small bar on each keycap. | `#ff5a36` |

The names in **keys** can be in any case:

| Type | To draw |
| --- | --- |
| `cmd`, `command` or `⌘` | ⌘ |
| `shift` or `⇧` | ⇧ |
| `opt`, `option`, `alt` or `⌥` | ⌥ |
| `ctrl`, `control` or `⌃` | ⌃ |
| `up`, `down`, `left`, `right` | An arrow |
| `plus` | The + key |
| One character | That character, in capitals |
| Anything longer, such as `esc` | That word on a wider keycap |

A long row of keys shrinks to fit the frame. A clip needs at least 25 frames plus 10 for each key to press them all before its exit, so a shortcut of seven or more keys wants longer than the default 90 frames.

[Shortcut overlays](/recording/shortcut-overlays/) places clips drawn by this template for you, from the shortcuts in a screen recording.

### Step badge

A numbered accent circle popping in at a corner, with a label panel wiping out from behind it.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **number** | The step's number, 0 to 99. | 1 |
| **label** | The panel's text. Leave it empty for no panel. | Open the export sheet |
| **position** | `top-left`, `top-right`, `bottom-left` or `bottom-right`. | `top-left` |
| **accent** | The circle. | `#ff5a36` |

### Window frame

A browser or app window's frame to sit over a screen recording: a title bar with three dots, a title or an address field, and a rounded outline. The inside stays clear.

Put the recording on a track below the frame, then use the [Picture sub-tab](/canvas/picture-tab/) to fit it inside the outline.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **style** | `browser` draws back and forward arrows and an address field. `window` draws a title. | `browser` |
| **title** | The title, shown with the `window` style. | Frame Job |
| **url** | The address, shown with the `browser` style. | example.com |
| **theme** | `dark` or `light`. | `dark` |
| **scale** | The share of the title-safe area the frame covers, 0.5 to 1. | 0.86 |
| **shadow** | Draws a soft shadow outside the outline. | On |
| **accent** | The address field's outline, so it has no effect with `window`. | `#ff5a36` |

The shadow is only as wide as the room around the frame, so it thins out as **scale** nears 1.

## Social & YouTube

### End screen

A card laid out for a video's end screen: a title, two 16:9 spaces and a subscribe circle. On a landscape or square canvas the two spaces sit side by side with the circle centred under them. On a portrait canvas they're a column.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **title** | The line at the top. Leave it empty for none. | Watch next |
| **videoA** | The label under the first space. | Latest video |
| **videoB** | The label under the second space. | Best for you |
| **subscribe** | The label under the circle. | Subscribe |
| **background** | The card's colour. | `#0b0b10` |
| **accent** | The play buttons and the circle's outline. | `#ff5a36` |

The spaces are placeholders drawn into the picture, for the video elements you add on the platform when you publish. They don't link to anything.

### Handle tag

An accent tile holding a glyph, with a pill showing your @handle expanding from behind it, at a corner.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **handle** | The text in the pill. Leave it empty for no pill. | @framejob |
| **glyph** | `play`, `camera`, `bubble`, `note` or `globe`. | `play` |
| **position** | `bottom-left`, `bottom-right`, `top-left` or `top-right`. | `bottom-left` |
| **accent** | The tile. | `#ff5a36` |

### Social popup

A notification card rising in at a corner, with a generic avatar and a handle. Its heart, speech bubble or share arrow pops as the count ticks up by one.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **kind** | `like` draws a heart, `comment` a speech bubble and `share` an arrow. | `like` |
| **handle** | The name on the card. | @framejob |
| **text** | The comment. It shows only with `comment`, at most three lines, and anything further is dropped. | Loved this edit! |
| **count** | The number it starts from, 0 or more. It ends one higher. | 1280 |
| **position** | `bottom-right`, `bottom-left`, `top-right` or `top-left`. | `bottom-right` |
| **accent** | The heart, bubble or arrow. | `#ff3b30` |

### Subscribe button

A button that a cursor glides to and clicks. It turns grey with a check and its done label, then a bell pops in beside it and rings after a second click.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **label** | The button before the click. | SUBSCRIBE |
| **doneLabel** | The button after it. | SUBSCRIBED |
| **bell** | Adds the bell and the second click. | On |
| **position** | `bottom`, `center` or `top`. | `bottom` |
| **accent** | The button before the click, and the bell once it's clicked. | `#e5322d` |

## Data & Time

The three charts read their data from text fields, with entries separated by commas:

- Values and labels pair up by position, and a value with no label has a blank one.
- An entry that isn't a number is dropped, so the values after it line up one label earlier.
- An empty entry, such as a comma at the end, counts as 0.

### Bar chart

Bars growing one after another on a dark card, each with its figure above it and its label below, under a title.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **values** | The bars' values, separated by commas. | 12, 19, 7, 24, 15, 30 |
| **labels** | The labels, separated by commas. | Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat |
| **title** | The line at the top. It isn't fitted, so keep it short. | Sessions per day |
| **color** | The bars. | `#2d7ff9` |

The tallest bar sets the scale, and the figures are rounded to whole numbers. The card's colour isn't a property.

### Countdown

A timer counting down or up, one step a second, inside a ring that empties or fills. The digits pulse on each tick, and the circle flashes in the accent colour at the end.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **from** | The starting number, in seconds. | 10 |
| **to** | The final number. Make it greater than **from** to count up. | 0 |
| **format** | `seconds` shows 90, `m:ss` shows 1:30 and `h:mm:ss` shows 0:01:30. | `m:ss` |
| **label** | The line above the digits, drawn in capitals. | Starting in |
| **ring** | Draws the ring. Without it the digits stay on their dark disc. | On |
| **accent** | The ring and the flash. | `#ff5a36` |

The count runs in real seconds whatever the frame rate, so the clip's length decides how far it gets. The default 330 frames is 11 seconds at 30 fps, which fits a ten-second count and the exit. At 60 fps that clip is 5.5 seconds, so lengthen it. For any longer count, lengthen the clip to the count plus a second.

With **from** and **to** equal there's nothing to count: the number stands in a full ring and nothing flashes.

### Counter

A number counting from one value to another with thousands separators, a prefix and a label under it. It starts fast and slows into its final number, then holds.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **from** | The starting number. | 0 |
| **to** | The final number. | 1284093 |
| **prefix** | Text right before the number. | $ |
| **label** | The line under it. | raised this year |
| **color** | The number and the label. | `#f5f5f0` |

The number shows no decimals, and it isn't shrunk to fit, so a very long one can run past the edges.

### Donut chart

Slices sweeping round a donut on a dark card, with the first slice's share counting up in the centre above its label, and a legend with each share. The legend sits beside the donut on a landscape or square canvas, and below it on a portrait one.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **values** | The slices' values, separated by commas. A value below 0 counts as 0. | 45, 30, 15, 10 |
| **labels** | The labels, separated by commas. | Video, Audio, Graphics, Other |
| **title** | The line at the top. | Where the time goes |
| **accent** | The first slice. | `#ff5a36` |

The other slices take colours from a fixed set of six, in order, repeating after the sixth. The shares are rounded to whole percentages, so they may not add up to exactly 100. The card's colour isn't a property.

### Gauge

A 270° dial with ticks, whose accent arc and needle swing to a value while the figure counts up.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **value** | The reading, held between **min** and **max**. | 72 |
| **min** | The start of the dial. | 0 |
| **max** | The end of the dial. At or below **min** it's taken as **min** plus 1. | 100 |
| **label** | The line inside the dial, drawn in capitals. | Speed |
| **units** | The line under the figure. | km/h |
| **accent** | The arc and the needle. | `#ff5a36` |

A whole **value** counts in whole numbers, and any other shows one decimal place.

### Line chart

A line drawn on through its points on a dark card, a dot popping at each one, with the last value called out in a pill.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **values** | The points, separated by commas. | 12, 18, 15, 26, 22, 34, 41 |
| **labels** | The labels along the bottom, separated by commas. | Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul |
| **title** | The line at the top. | Monthly active users |
| **area** | Shades the area under the line. | On |
| **accent** | The line, the dots' outlines, the shading and the pill. | `#2d7ff9` |

The scale runs from 0, or from the lowest value if that's negative, up to the highest. A single value draws one dot and no line. The card's colour isn't a property.

### Progress bar

A labelled bar on a dark plate, filling to a percentage with the figure counting, and a shine sweeping across it once it has filled.

| Property | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **value** | How far it fills, 0 to 100. | 68 |
| **label** | The text above the bar. | Rendering |
| **showPercent** | Shows the percentage at the right. | On |
| **position** | `bottom`, `center` or `top`. | `bottom` |
| **accent** | The fill. | `#2d7ff9` |

## Any canvas shape

Templates size themselves by the shorter side of the size they draw at, which is the canvas's unless you change it, so nothing grows too large on a tall project.

Seventeen of them are laid out for all three shapes. Everything they draw stays inside the title-safe area, the frame less 5 % on every side, at 1920 × 1080, 1080 × 1920 and 1080 × 1080. Their entrances are a spring, a wipe or a fade, and never a move in from outside the frame. Long text is fitted or wrapped to stay inside, down to a smallest size for each text, so a paragraph typed where a title belongs can still run over.

The other six are Bar chart, Callout, Counter, Kinetic type, Lower third and Title card. They don't keep to the title-safe area, and a left-aligned Lower third enters from beyond the frame's left edge. Once settled, with their default text, their words sit at least 24 pixels inside a 1080 × 1920 frame. Only Title card fits its title and Kinetic type wraps its text, so check your own words on a tall canvas. [Safe areas](/canvas/safe-areas/) shows the title-safe guide in the preview.

A square canvas takes the landscape layout.

## Templates end clean

The same seventeen each have an accent colour and leave in about their last 15 frames, so the last frame is bare. An overlay ends clear, and a card ends on its background alone. A card's background never animates: only what's on it moves or fades.

The other six end their own way:

| Template | At the end of the clip |
| --- | --- |
| Lower third | Fades out over the last 15 frames and ends clear. |
| Title card | Its text fades over the last 18 frames, leaving the gradient. |
| Bar chart, Callout, Counter, Kinetic type | Hold to the last frame and stop with the clip. |

## Template fonts and glyphs

Templates draw only with fonts that come with Frame Job, so a project looks the same on any Mac. The font isn't a property. To change it, [edit the graphic's code](/graphics/edit-a-module/).

| Used for | Font |
| --- | --- |
| Text in most templates | Montserrat, extra bold or black |
| Digits that tick, the shares in Donut chart's legend, Counter's label and the figures in Bar chart | IBM Plex Mono |
| Window frame's title and address | Source Sans 3, semibold |
| Title card's title | Anton |
| Title card's kicker and Bar chart's labels | Bebas Neue |
| Callout's label | Darker Grotesque |

Every symbol is a generic shape drawn by the template, never a brand's artwork: the keycap symbols, the heart, the bell, the play triangle and the glyphs in Handle tag. None of them is a platform's own logo.

## Good to know

- Each insert copies the template into the project under a name not yet in use, such as `lower-third`, then `lower-third-2`. Undo takes the clip away, and the copy stays until you [delete it](/graphics/manage-graphics/).
- Your copy is yours to change, and it stays as it was when you inserted it. [Graphics in a vertical version](/graphics/vertical-graphics/) covers the one exception.
- Most properties can [change over time with an action](/graphics/animate-properties/). These keep one value for the whole clip: **number** in Chapter card and Step badge, **values** and **labels** in Donut chart and Line chart, **keys** in Keyboard keys, **items** in News ticker and **count** in Social popup.

## With an agent

[`list_graphics`](/mcp/tools-graphics/#list_graphics) lists the templates by category, as the sheet does. [`insert_graphic`](/mcp/tools-graphics/#insert_graphic) places one at the playhead: give it the name `list_graphics` shows, such as `arrow-highlight`, with `from` set to `template`. The reply names the project's copy, which may be `arrow-highlight-2`. To fill in the properties afterwards, use [`set_graphic`](/mcp/tools-graphics/#set_graphic), with the names in the tables above. See [Tools: graphics](/mcp/tools-graphics/).
