The Transcript tab marks each long pause in the speech. Click one and delete it to tighten the pause without taking any words, as one undo step.

## Pauses in the transcript

Where the speech stops for more than a second between two words of one clip, the transcript shows the pause's length in grey after the word before it, such as `[2.4 s]`.

The length is what the voice model hears as silence between that word and the next. The model is the one [Remove Silences](/cleanup/remove-silences/) uses. It runs when the project opens the file, so a file's pauses appear once it has finished. They appear for words from an [AssemblyAI transcript](/captions/assemblyai-transcription/) as well as words read on this Mac.

That second is measured as the clip plays on the timeline, so a clip's speed changes what counts. A 0.8 s silence in a clip slowed to half speed plays for 1.6 s, and shows as a pause.

## Select a pause

Click the pause. It's selected on its own, the part that would go becomes the timeline's [in/out range](/timeline/in-out-range/), and the playhead moves to the start of that cut. To hear what would go, turn on [Loop](/timeline/loop/).

<kbd>⎋</kbd> clears the selection and the range. Dragging, <kbd>⇧</kbd> and <kbd>⌘A</kbd> select words only, so a pause is always selected by clicking it.

## Delete the pause

<Steps>

1. Choose **View ▸ Transcript** (<kbd>⌃⌘T</kbd>) to open the Transcript tab.

2. Click the pause, the grey length in the text.

3. Press <kbd>⌫</kbd>.

</Steps>

The pause is shortened, keeping 0.2 s at each end, and the gap closes on every unlocked track, as one undo step named **Delete Pause**. A `[2.4 s]` pause loses about 2 s and leaves about 0.4 s. The range clears and the playhead lands where the cut was.

<kbd>⌦</kbd>, <kbd>⌘⌫</kbd>, **Edit ▸ Delete** and **Edit ▸ Ripple Delete** do the same, as long as the transcript text has the keyboard.

The cut lands on whole frames and never takes the start of a word.

The tab won't cut a pause when a locked track has words inside the cut, or when the cut would pass the limit of 4,096 clips. It beeps and shows the same [reason](/captions/delete-words/#when-the-tab-wont-cut) it gives for words, at the foot of the inspector.

## When a pause isn't shown

The tab shows a pause only when it can cut it cleanly. There's no pause marked:

- between two clips, including the two halves of a clip you've split
- when either word next to it is [faint](/captions/delete-words/#words-that-cant-be-cut): inside a nest, under a transition or on a locked track
- when a word on another track, one the tab shows, reaches into the part that would go, because the cut would take that word
- when a clip whose words haven't been read yet starts inside the cut
- when keeping 0.2 s at each end would leave nothing to cut

## With an agent

`get_timeline_transcript` reads the same pauses, each with the range the tab would cut it by, and one [`ripple_delete_ranges`](/mcp/tools-cut-and-delete/#ripple_delete_ranges) call shortens one. `set_range` can shade that range first. An agent can pass several in that call, which makes them one undo step. See [Cut words and pauses](/mcp/tools-captions/#cut-words-and-pauses).
