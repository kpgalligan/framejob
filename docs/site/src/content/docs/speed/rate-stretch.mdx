A rate stretch changes a clip's length by changing its speed, not by trimming it. The clip still plays all of the source it showed, faster or slower, so it's the quick way to make a shot fit by eye.

## Stretch a clip

<Steps>

1. Hold <kbd>⌥</kbd>.

2. Press on the **end** edge of a clip of a video or audio file, and drag. The timeline shows the result as you drag.

3. Let go.

</Steps>

The stretch lands as one undo step, **Rate Stretch**. The new rate is the length of source the clip shows divided by its new length, so a 10 s clip at ×1 dragged to 5 s plays at ×2.

Frame Job reads <kbd>⌥</kbd> when you press, so hold it before the drag starts. <kbd>⌥⇧</kbd> on an edge is a [roll](/editing/slip-and-roll/), not a stretch.

Actions keep their fraction of the clip. Captions over the clip are re-timed in the same step, which is then named **Rate Stretch and Re-time Captions**, and they jump when you let go. See [Captions follow a speed change](/speed/captions-and-speed/).

## Snapping

A rate stretch snaps like any other edge drag. Hold <kbd>⌘</kbd> during the drag to turn snapping off for it. See [Snapping](/timeline/snapping/).

## Which edges and clips stretch

| You drag with ⌥ | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A media clip's end edge | A rate stretch |
| A clip's start edge | An ordinary trim |
| The end edge of a still, text, shape, graphic or any other clip | An ordinary trim, with the undo step **Trim Clip** |
| An edge on a locked track | Nothing. The edge doesn't move. |
| The end edge of a clip linked to its detached sound | Nothing, with no beep and no reason, until you [attach it](/audio/detach-and-attach/) |

## Where a stretch stops

The clip stops following the drag when the next step would break a limit, and it lands at the last length that worked:

- **Rate limits.** The stretch can't need a rate outside ×0.1 – ×1000.
- **One frame.** The clip can't get shorter than one frame.
- **The next clip.** A stretch into the next clip follows the same [overlap rule](/speed/change-speed/#a-slowed-clip-overlapping-the-next) as any slowed clip, so the later clip gains a transition across an allowed overlap.

Drag back inside the limit and the clip follows you again.

## A ramped clip

On a clip with a speed ramp, the whole ramp stretches with the clip and keeps its shape. The stretch stops where any of its rates would pass the limits. See [Edit a ramped clip](/speed/edit-a-ramped-clip/).

## Stretching past ×12

If a stretch takes a clip with sound past ×12, Frame Job puts the stretch back and asks first.

- **Set Speed** applies the stretch you made.
- **Cancel** leaves the clip as it was.

See [Sound at high speed](/speed/sound-at-high-speed/).

## With an agent

`rate_stretch` takes `clip` and `to_end`, the clip's new end on the timeline, and both are required. A text, shape, image or freeze clip is an error. The reply's `captions_retimed` lists the captions that moved, and `captions_removed` those whose speech the clip no longer plays. See [`rate_stretch`](/mcp/tools-speed/#rate_stretch).
