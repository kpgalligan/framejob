## Sound is left out above ×12

The limit is the same with [Keep pitch](/speed/keep-pitch/) on or off. At exactly ×12 the sound still plays, and at anything faster it doesn't. The picture carries on to the top of the speed range, ×1000.

The limit is fixed, and no setting changes it. It sits a margin below the speeds at which an export with sound can stall.

Nothing is deleted. Bring the clip back to ×12 or below and its sound returns.

## The alert

Before an edit takes a clip with sound from ×12 or below to above ×12, an alert asks first. For one clip it reads:

> “Interview” will be silent at ×16.
>
> Frame Job leaves a clip’s audio out above ×12. The picture still plays.

For several clips it counts them. It reads “3 clips will be silent at ×16.”, or “3 clips will be silent at their new speeds.” when their rates differ, and then “Frame Job leaves a clip’s audio out above ×12. The pictures still play.”

The alert is a question, not a block. You can always go ahead.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Set Speed** | Makes the edit exactly as you asked. It's the default button, and it keeps this name when the edit is a ramp or a stretch. |
| **Cancel** | Changes nothing and adds no undo step. |

You're asked once. A clip that's already above ×12 isn't asked about again, even if you make it faster still. A clip whose sound is switched off, or whose file has no sound, isn't asked about at all.

## Where the alert appears

| You're using | If you choose **Cancel** |
| --- | --- |
| **Clip ▸ Speed…** | The sheet stays open. |
| **Clip ▸ Speed Ramp…** | The sheet stays open. |
| **Clip ▸ Retime Range…** | The sheet stays open. |
| **Clip ▸ Speed Up Typing…** | The sheet stays open. |
| The [Speed section](/speed/speed-section/): **Rate**, its quick picks, **Keep pitch**, **Reset** or **Fit to** | The fields go back to what they showed. |
| The [⌥ rate stretch](/speed/rate-stretch/) | The clip stays as it was before the drag. |

A stretch is put back when you let go, so nothing has changed while the question is up. **Set Speed** then applies it again.

Moving, resizing or removing an action can't raise a clip's fastest rate, so those edits never ask.

## Ramps and the ×12 rule

On a [ramped clip](/speed/speed-ramps/), Frame Job looks at the fastest rate set anywhere on the clip. If any rate in the ramp is above ×12, the whole clip is silent, not only the fast part.

The alert counts the ramp's peak rate in the same way. A clip at ×1 with one Speed action ending at ×16 is asked about at ×16.

An end rate on an action lying past the clip's end still counts, even though that part never plays. If a trimmed clip has gone silent for no clear reason, check the count in the header of its [action lane](/motion/action-lane/) in the Clip tab. An action wholly past the clip's end has no band you can reach, but it's still counted.

## The silent audio note

The Speed section shows **Audio is silent above ×12** while a selected clip is past the limit. It needs a clip whose file has sound and whose sound is on. It appears in either pitch mode, and a ramp counts by its fastest rate.

## Good to know

- A clip that only has sound follows the same rule, and is silent above ×12.
- Clips inside a [nest](/editing/nests/) count too.
- Turning a clip's sound back on while it's above ×12 doesn't bring up the alert, because the speed didn't change. The silent audio note shows instead.
- When Speed Up Typing runs by itself after a [recording lands](/recording/when-it-lands/), Frame Job can't stop to ask. A speed-up that would silence a clip isn't made, and the reason appears at the foot of the inspector.

## With an agent

An agent is never asked. Its edit goes through, and the result lists the ids of the clips that went silent in `audio_left_out`, with a sentence saying so in `audio_note`. Neither appears if no clip crossed the limit. See [Above ×12 the sound is left out](/mcp/tools-speed/#above-12-the-sound-is-left-out).
