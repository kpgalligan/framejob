The Speed sheet plays the selected clips at one new constant rate, and shows you the length that results before you apply it.

## Set a new speed

<Steps>

1. Select one or more clips of a video or audio file.

2. Choose **Clip ▸ Speed…**. The item has no keyboard shortcut.

3. Type a **Rate**, or pick one from **Presets**.

4. Read the [readout](#the-readout) under the controls. It updates when you pick a preset, change **Preserve pitch** or leave the **Rate** field.

5. Choose **Apply**.

</Steps>

**Clip ▸ Speed…** is available when the selection holds at least one media clip, meaning a clip of a video or audio file. Clips of other kinds in the selection are skipped. See [Which clips take speed](/speed/overview/#which-clips-take-speed).

To change one rate without opening a sheet, use **Rate** in the [Speed section](/speed/speed-section/).

## The Speed sheet

| Control | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rate:** | The playback rate as a number: 1 is normal, 2 is twice as fast, 0.5 is half speed. It takes up to three decimal places, and the field won't accept a value outside ×0.1 – ×1000, the range printed beside the sheet's buttons. | The rate the selected clips share, or ×1 if they differ |
| **Presets** | Fills in **Rate** with 0.25×, 0.50×, 1×, 1.5×, 2×, 4×, 10× or 100×. The same list appears on the [Speed Ramp](/speed/make-a-ramp/) and [Retime Range](/speed/retime-range/) sheets. | |
| **Preserve pitch** | Keeps the sound at its own pitch at the new speed. Turn it off and the pitch rises and falls with the speed, like tape. See [Keep pitch](/speed/keep-pitch/). | The first selected clip's setting, which is on for a new clip |

## The readout

Frame Job tries the change before you commit to it, so the readout shows what **Apply** will do:

- The clip count and the total length before and after, for example `3 clips · 00:00:12:00 → 00:00:06:00`.
- One line for each clip whose transition starts playing, gets longer or gets shorter:
  - `clip 7 gains a 0.67 s cross dissolve`
  - `clip 7's cross dissolve grows to 1.50 s`
  - `clip 7's cross dissolve shrinks to 0.67 s`
- When a selected clip has a speed ramp, "Each speed ramp keeps its shape: the rates it names scale with the base rate."

A clip that couldn't take the new rate counts at its old length, and the rest of the selection still counts.

## Apply the change

**Apply** sets the rate on every listed clip as one undo step, **Set Speed**. Captions the change re-times are part of the same step, which is then named **Set Speed and Re-time Captions**. See [Captions follow a speed change](/speed/captions-and-speed/).

If nothing would change, **Apply** closes the sheet and adds no undo step.

If the rate takes a clip with sound above ×12, an alert comes first, such as "“interview.mov” will be silent at ×20."

- **Set Speed** closes the sheet and applies the change.
- **Cancel** leaves the sheet up with nothing applied.

See [Sound at high speed](/speed/sound-at-high-speed/).

## What a speed change does

- **The clip's start stays and its length changes.** A faster clip can leave a gap after it, and later clips don't move up to fill it. [Close Gap](/editing/close-gaps/) closes it.
- **The new length snaps to a whole frame**, and never runs past the end of the file at the new rate. See [The new length](/speed/fit-to-duration/#the-new-length).
- **Actions keep their places** as fractions of the clip's length.
- **On a ramped clip** the sheet sets the clip's own rate, its base rate, and scales the whole ramp with it. See [Edit a ramped clip](/speed/edit-a-ramped-clip/).

A speed ramp works the other way round: it leaves the clip where it is and changes how much of the source plays.

## A slowed clip overlapping the next

A slower clip gets longer, so it can run into the clip after it. Later clips stay where they are, so the two now overlap.

- **If the overlap is allowed**, the later clip gets a transition across it, and the readout names that transition.
- **If it isn't allowed**, the slowed clip keeps its old speed. Any other selected clips still change.

An overlap is allowed up to half the length of the shorter clip. It isn't allowed when one of the clips is an adjustment clip, or when the clip would reach a second neighbour on the same side. See [Transitions](/motion/transitions/).

## Good to know

- **Clips at different rates.** The field opens on ×1, and **Apply** sets every listed clip to the rate in the field, even if you only changed **Preserve pitch**. Choose **Cancel** to leave them at their own rates.
- **Preserve pitch** applies to every listed clip, and changing it alone counts as a change.
- **Linked clips.** Frame Job won't change the speed of a clip linked to its detached sound until you attach it. See [Linked clips](/speed/overview/#linked-clips).
- **Inside a nest**, the sheet works on the clips you've selected in the open nest.

## With an agent

`set_speed` does the same job, one clip at a time. Give it `clip` and exactly one of `rate` or `duration`, the length that [Fit to](/speed/fit-to-duration/) takes. Both or neither is an error, and so is a rate of zero or less. Leave out `keep_pitch` and the clip's setting stays as it is.

An agent isn't asked about ×12: the reply says which clips lost their sound. Its undo step is named **Speed**. See [`set_speed`](/mcp/tools-speed/#set_speed).
